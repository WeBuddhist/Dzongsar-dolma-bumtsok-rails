# Essence of the Two Accumulations: The Ritual for the Elaborate Outer Practice from the Mind Treasure, “Tārā’s Profound Essence

## A fifteen-verse prayer to the King of the Śākyas

lama dang jampé yang la güpé chagtsal lo
In devotion I prostrate to the guru and Mañjughoṣa.

gang zhik jangchub shingwang drung du dü dé pung ché sa dang kha la künkhyab ching
When you sat under the great Bodhi Tree and the earth and sky were filled with hordes of māras,
dorjé dungtung dang ni khorlo mé dang ri dang gangri trengwé char bebpa
Raining down cascades of vajras, spears, wheels,fire, and entire mountains and glaciers—
dzutrül nampar trül jé tobden metok da chen jangchub dok chir tsönpa na
Magical displays unleashed by the mighty Flower-Arrowed One striving to avert your awakening—
jampé tuk kyi legpar pam dzé dzogpé sangyé gang nyé délagö chak tsal
You defeated them all with nothing but love in your heart and attained perfect buddhahood – to you I bow my head.

khyö kyi zhen gyi dön lé du
When, for the benefit of others
sam dang jorwa püntsogpé
And with perfect intention and application,
jangchub dampar tugkyé tsé
You gave rise to the noble resolve to attain supreme awakening,
sa yang nampa druk tu yö
Even the earth shook in six motions.

jin dang tsültrim lek drub shing
You mastered generosity and discipline,
zö dang tsöndrü gompar dzé
Cultivated forbearance and diligence,
samten sherab tar chinpé
And broughtmeditation and wisdom to their culmination—
paröl chin dzok khyö chak tsal
Homage to you, in whomthe perfections were complete.

drogön dangpo tampa na
Protector of beings, the moment you were born,
jigten khepa tamché ga
The learned and wise of this world were delighted,
dü nam shintu dogpa kyé
Māras grew deeply concerned,
mutek jepa tadak trak
And all those who proclaimed mistaken paths were terrified.

dé lé khyö kyi tenpa ni
And so, the activity of your teaching
nyimé ö zhin trinlé nga
Shines forthlike the light of the sun;
nampa kün tu mi luwé
And being never deceiving in any way,
tsalak khyö la dak kyab chi
You are the true friend in whomI take refuge.

khyö kyi sungrab sengé dra
When your sublime teaching, like the lion’s roar,
lhar ché jigten dragpa na
Resounded throughout this world with its gods,
tsang dang khyabjuk kugpar gyur
Brahmā and Viṣṇu were speechless,
lha yi lama wa tar zhum
And the teacher of the godsowered like a fox.

tubpa kang mik drogkhar dang
Sages, like Akṣapāda and Vālmīki,
drangsong gyepa né jok sok
Seers, such as Vyāsa and Vāsiṣṭha,
dragpa tobpa dedak kün
And many others of great renown —
chö kyi pam dzé khyö la dü
To you, who disarmed them all with the Dharma, I bow.

khyö kyi jangchub chok nyé né
Having attained supreme awakening,
drowé dön dzé ngotsar ché
You accomplished beings’ benefit in the most wondrous ways;
sipé sélema drölwar
And for as long as you were not yet free from the thirst for existence,
kusok tangwe bapu dang
You gave your body and life – this fills me with awe!

nyi kyi kusok mi zigpar
Without regard for your own well-being
khyé la penpar ja’o zhé
You proclaimed: “I will benefit you!”,
gönmé dagchak jé zungwa
And so you hold us, who are without protection, in your care.
gönpo khyö la dak kyab chi
Protector, in you I take refuge.

gyalwé dü dé tülwa dang
Victorious One, you tamed the hosts of māras,
mutek malü pam dzé dang
Disarmed all those who proclaimed mistaken paths,
nyentö lobma drangmé tröl
Liberated countless śrāvaka disciples,
gyalsé dampa lungten dzé
And bestowed prophecies upon sublime bodhisattvas.

déndré legtsok tongwa nam
Those who perceive such abundance of excellence
khyö kyi yönten dön nyerwé
Strive to accomplish your sublime qualities;
jangchub lédu jugpa dak
And so I, who follow the path to awakening,
laryang khyö la dak kyab chi
Once again take refuge in you.

khyö kyi sé ni jampéyang
You who made Mañjughoṣa your principal heir,
lobma sharadati bu
Śāriputra your foremost disciple,
jampa gyaltsab dampar dzé
And Maitreya your sublime regent;
semshé su zhik tetsom za
What wise person could ever doubt you!

khyö kyi yönten möchi tsal
What need is there to speak of your sublime qualities,
sé kyi yönten tongwé khum
When just to see those of your heirs is measure enough?
kyareng sharwé ziji kyi
For similarly does the splendor of the breaking dawn
pemö nyen gyi özer go
Announce the radiance of the lotus-opening sun.

jitawa dang jinyé chö
Having gained wisdom complete in every aspect—
khyö kyi nampa kün nyepé
Knowing the true nature of phenomena as well as their full extent—
zhepé trinlé dön zhin du
All the enlightened deeds you had undertaken,
drubpa khyö la dak chak tsal
You accomplishedperfectly;to you I pay homage.

detar drowé lama shakyé rik
Through this praise of the perfect buddha bhagavān,
buram shingpé dungyü lé trungpa
The teacher of beings from the Śākya clan,
chomden dzogpé sangyé töpa yi
Born into the line of Ikṣvāku,
drowé künkhyen nyur du tobpar shok
May all beings swiftly attain omniscience.

<small>This prayer in fifteen stanzas to the Bhagavān King of Śākyas was composed by the glorious Sakya Paṇḍita at the Tsuklagkhang in Lhasa. Translated by Christian Bernert.</small>

## A Garland of Utpala Blossoms: The Lineage Prayer for Zaptik Drolchok, the Mind Treasure of Tārā

chöku sherchin chomden kunzangmo
Buddha Samantabhadrī, dharmakāya’s Transcendent Wisdom,
longchö yongdzok benza varahi
Vajravārāhī, sambhogakāya’s total perfection;
trulpé namrol jetsün drolma yum
Sublime mother Tara, nirmāṇakāya’s magical display;
solwa deb so choktün ngödrub tsol
To you I pray, grant me siddhis, common and supreme!

sangye nyipa pema kara dang
Padmākara, our ‘second buddha’,
sang ngak dzö dzin daki bum gyi jé
Master of the treasury of secret mantra, lord over a hundred thousand ḍākinīs,
changchub sempa lhasé rolpa tsal
Bodhisattva prince Yeshé Rolpa Tsal,
solwa deb so choktün ngödrub tsol
To you I pray, grant me siddhis, common and supreme!

zabter gojé chokgyur dechen ling
Chokgyur Dechen Lingpa, revealer of profound termas;
kabab dün den dorjé ziji tsal
Dorjé Ziji Tsal, holder of the seven direct blessings;
gyalten sal dzé pema gar gyi wang
Pema Garwang, elucidator of the buddhas’ teachings;
solwa deb so choktün ngödrub tsol
To you I pray, grant me siddhis, common and supreme!

beró namtrul gyurmé tsewang tsal
kunzig jamyang chökyi lodö dang
mindrol tsolzé khyabdak manga lar
solwa deb so choktün ngödrub tsol
To you I pray, grant me siddhis, common and supreme!

zhenyang zabgyé mindrol dzö dzinpé
Holders of the treasury of ripening and liberation, vast and profound,
tsa gyü lama pakma nyur dzé lha
Root and lineage gurus, deities of the Sublime Lady, swift to act,
tsa sum dorjé chösung gyatso la
Infinite gurus, devas and ḍākinīs, with Vajra Dharma protectors,
solwa deb so choktün ngödrub tsol
To you I pray, grant me siddhis, common and supreme!

ngepar jungwé lo gyü zhingsa len
Let the earth of my mind be soaked in renunciation,
lhaksam dakpé jang trung tsar du ngar
Letting the green shoots of pure altruistic intention sprout and grow,
yeshe chenpö drebu yur zawé
Their branches laden with the fruits of great primordial wisdom,
dön nyi lhün gyi drubpar dzé du sol
That are the benefit of self and others, spontaneously fulfilled!

## The Essence of the Two Accumulations: The Ritual for the Elaborate Outer Practice
From the mind Treasure cycle The Profound Essence of Tara

Namo Gurvya aryatarayai!

<small>In honor of Tara, mother to all the guides</small>
<small>And their heirs in all three times—</small>
<small>She who has wonderful resolve and activity—</small>
<small>I set out here the practice according to Kriya and Carya.</small>

<small>Since this Profound Essence is a condensation of every quintessential profundity, it is not necessary to resort to much elaboration. Nevertheless there are two degrees of detail: one slightly elaborate and the other as a daily practice.</small>

<small>For the first, in front of a painting or statue of the noble Lady Tara, or whatever image of the Three Jewels you can acquire, arrange, on a clean surface, the two waters, the general offerings, the round white torma, the cleansing water, the offering mandala, grain for tossing, and other articles. If you can, keep the conduct of bathing and cleanliness. If unable to do that, at least perform the practice when you have not consumed meat or alcohol, such as in the morning. With an attitude of renunciation and bodhicitta, recite these verses of refuge and bodhicitta:</small>

dün gyi namkhar jetsünma ngönsum du zhukpar gyur
In the sky before me, Noble Tara is present in actuality.

namo, könchok kün ngö jetsünmar
Namo. In the Noble Lady, embodiment of all the Three Jewels,
dak sok dro kün kyap su chi
I and all beings take refuge.
jangchup mönpé semkyé né
Arousing bodhicitta in aspiration,
zapmö lam la jukpar gyi
I will enter the profound path!

<small>To gather the accumulations, recite:</small>

jetsün pakma drolma dang
To the noble Lady Tara,
chok chu dü sum zhukpa yi
And to all the conquerors and their heirs
gyalwa sé ché tamché la
Who reside in the ten directions and three times,
küné dangwé chak gyi o
Sincerely and with delight, I pay homage.

metok dukpö marmé dri
I offer flowers and incense, lamps and perfume,
zhalzé rolmo lasokpa
Food, music and the like,
ngöjor yi kyi trül né bül
Both materially present and mentally created.
pakmé tsok nam zhé su sol
May they be accepted by the Noble Lady’s assembly!

tokma mé né danté bar
Throughout beginningless time until this very day,
mi gé chu dang tsamé nga
I have committed the ten non-virtues and the five without interval.
sem ni nyönmong wang gyurpé
I apologize for each and every evil deed committed
dikpa tamché shakpar gyi
Under the influence of disturbed states of mind.

nyentö rangyal jangchup sem
I rejoice in all merit and goodness
soso kyewo lasokpé
Created throughout the three times
dü sum gewa chi sakpé
By shravakas, pratyeka-buddhas and bodhisattvas,
sönam la ni dak yi rang
By ordinary beings, and all others.

semchen nam kyi sampa dang
In accordance with the capabilities
lo yi jedrak jitawar
And individual aspirations of sentient beings,
chechung tünmong tekpa yi
I ask you to turn the wheel of Dharma
chö kyi khorlo kor du sol
Of the general, greater and lesser vehicles.

khorwa jisi matong bar
Until samsara has been emptied,
nya ngen mi da tukjé yi
Do not pass into nirvana, but please, in your compassion,
dukngal gyamtsor jingwa yi
Care for each and every being
semchen nam la zik su sol
Sinking in the ocean of suffering.

dak gi sönam chi sakpa
May whatever merit I have gathered
tamché jangchup gyur gyur né
Become a cause for enlightenment,
ringpor mi tok drowa yi
And may I, in the near future,
drenpé pal du dak gyur chik
Become a splendid guide for sentient beings.

<small>To bless the offerings:</small>

om benza amrita kundali hana hana hung pé

om sobhava shuddho sarva dharma sobhava shuddho hang

<small>Thereby cleanse and purify.</small>

neyül pemo köpé zhing
Here, in the Realm of Lotus Array,
yip dang gyen kö tsemepar
Perfectly formed and abundantly decorated
lhadzé tingdzin lé drupé
There are offerings surpassing thought—
chödzé sam mi khyapar gyur
Divine articles created from samadhi.

om benza argham padam pushpé düpé aloké gendhé naividyé shapda ah hung

om benza saparana kham

<small>Following that, begin with honoring and making offerings to the Three Jewels in general:</small>

chok chu dü zhi lama gyelwa sé dang chepa tamché dün gyi namkhar benza sa ma dza
The masters, the conquerors, and their heirs of the ten directions and four times are in the sky before me, vajra-samaja!

malü semchen kün gyi gön gyur ching
Protector of all sentient beings without exception,
dü dé pung ché mi zé jom dzé lha
Deity who defeats the endless hordes of maras,
ngö nam malü jizhin khyen gyurpé
Since you are fully omniscient,
chomden khor ché né dir shek su sol
Blessed One and your retinue, please approach this place!

pema kamala ya stam

<small>Next recite the Seven Branch Prayer:</small>

ji nyé su dak chok chü jikten na
To all the buddhas, lions of men,
dü sum shekpa mi yi sengé kün
In all directions of the universe, through past and present and future:
dak gi malü dé dak tamché la
To every single one of you, I bow in homage;
lü dang ngak yi dangwé chak gyi-o
Devotion fills my body, speech, and heart.

zangpo chöpé mönlam top dak gi
Through the power of this prayer, Aspiring to Good Action,
gyelwa tamché yi kyi ngönsum du
All the victorious ones appear, vivid before my mind,
zhing gi dül nyé lü rap tüpa yi
And I multiply my body as many times as there are atoms in the universe,
gyelwa kün la rap tu chaktsel lo
Each one bowing in prostration to all the buddhas.

dül chik teng na dül nyé sangyé nam
In every atom preside as many buddhas as there are atoms,
sangyé sé kyi ü na zhukpa dak
And around them, all their bodhisattva heirs,
dé tar chö kyi ying nam malüpa
And so I imagine them filling completely
tamché gyelwa dak gi gangwar mö
The entire space of reality.

dé dak ngakpa mizé gyamtso nam
Saluting them with an endless ocean of praise,
yang kyi yenlak gyamtso dra kün gyi
An ocean of sounds of different melodies,
gyelwa kün gyi yönten rap jö ching
I sing of the buddhas’ noble qualities
dewar shekpa tamché dak gi tö
And praise all those who have gone to perfect bliss.

métok dampa trengwa dampa dang
To every buddha, I make offerings:
silnyen nam dang jukpa duk chok dang
Of the loveliest flowers, of beautiful garlands,
marmé chok dang dukpö dampa yi
Of music and perfumed ointments, the best of parasols,
gyelwa dé dak la ni chöpar gyi
The brightest lamps, and finest incense.

naza dampa nam dang dri chok dang
To every buddha, I make offerings:
chéma purma rirap nyampa dang
Exquisite garments and the most fragrant scents,
köpa khyépar pakpé chok kün gyi
Powdered incense, heaped as high as Mount Meru,
gyelwa dé dak la ni chöpar gyi
And all arranged in perfect symmetry.

chöpa gang nam lamé gyachewa
Then the vast and unsurpassable offering—
dé dak gyelwa tamché la yang mö
Inspired by my devotion to all the buddhas, and
zangpo chö la dépé top dak gi
Moved by the power of my faith in Good Actions—
gyelwa kün la chaktsel chöpar gyi
I prostrate in offering to all you victorious ones.

döchak zhédang timuk wang gi ni
Whatever negative acts I have committed,
lü dang ngak dang dézhin yi kyi kyang
While driven by desire, anger, and ignorance,
dikpa dak gi gyipa chi chipa
With my body, my speech, and my mind,
dé dak tamché dak gi sosor shak
Before you, I confess and purify each and every one.

chok chü gyelwa kün dang sangyé sé
With a heart full of delight, I rejoice at all the merits
rangyel nam dang lop dang mi lop dang
Of buddhas and bodhisattvas,
drowa kün gyi sönam gang la yang
Pratyeka-buddhas, those in training, and arhats beyond training,
dé dak kün gyi jésu dak yirang
And every living being, throughout the entire universe.

gang nam chok chü jikten drönma nam
You who are like beacons of light shining through the worlds,
jangchup rimpar sangyé ma chak nyé
Who passed through the stages of enlightenment to attain buddhahood, from all
attachment free at last—
gönpo dé dak dak gi tamché la
Protectors, I exhort you all:
khorlo lana mépar korwar kül
Turn the unsurpassable wheel of Dharma!

nyangen datön gang zhé dé dak la
Joining my palms together,
drowa kün la pen zhing dewé chir
I pray to you who intend to pass into nirvana,
kelpa zhing gi dül nyé zhukpar yang
Please remain, for as many aeons as there are atoms in this world,
dak gi telmo rap jar sölwar gyi
And bring well-being and happiness to us all!

chaktselwa dang chö ching shakpa dang
What little virtue I have gathered through my homage,
jésu yirang kül zhing sölwa yi
Offering, confession, and rejoicing,
gewa chung zé dak gi chi sakpa
Through my exhortation, and prayer—
tamché dak gi jangchup chir ngo
I dedicate it all to the enlightenment of beings!

<small>And thus:</small>

om ah hung༔ dak dang tayé semchen gyi༔
Om ah hung. On behalf of all beings, I offer༔
lü dang longchö getsok kün༔
All our bodies, possessions, and sources of merit,༔
ling zhi rirap nyidar ché༔
The four continents, Mount Meru, the sun and the moon,༔
lhami longchö sam mikhyap༔
All the unimaginable delights of gods and humans.༔

künzang chöpé trinpung ché༔
Continuously manifesting all of these༔
gyün mi chepar trül jé té༔
As Samantabhadra offering-clouds༔
könchok rinchen tsawa sum༔
To the deities of the Three Jewels and the Three Roots,༔
chösung norlha gyamtso la༔
And to the ocean of Dharma protectors and wealth deities—༔
güpé taktu bülwar gyi༔
Continuously I offer them, with devotion.༔
sönam tsokchen rapdzok né༔
May our great accumulation of merit be perfected༔
yeshé nangwa gyepar shok༔
And the light of wisdom illuminate the world!༔

om guru deva dakini sarva ratna mandala pudza megha ah hung༔

<small>With this, offer the mandala.</small>

namo, sangyé chö dang gendün dang༔
Namo. Buddha, Dharma and Sangha,༔
lama yidam khandro dang༔
Guru, yidam and dakini,༔
chösung norlha ter gyi dak༔
Dharma guardians, wealth gods and Treasure lords—༔
yeshé chik gi ngowo lé༔
From an identical wisdom-essence༔

rangzhin magak chiryang tön༔
The unobscured nature can appear as anything at all,༔
tukjé trinlé mepo ché༔
With the capacity for wondrous enlightened activity.༔
dro kün kyap dang gön gyurpa༔
To you, our refuge and the protectors of all beings,༔
khyentsé daknyi khyé nam la༔
To all of you, sovereigns of wisdom and compassion,༔

chaktsal nying né kyap su chi༔
I pay homage and take heartfelt refuge in you.༔
lü dang longchö chöpar bül༔
I present my body and wealth as offerings.༔
dak dang tayé semchen nam༔
With your compassion please always protect me༔
taktu tukjé kyap dzé sol༔
And the many countless other sentient beings.༔

né dang nöpa zhiwa dang༔
Pacify illness and evil influences;༔
tsé dang sönam pelwa dang༔
Increase our lifespan and merits;༔
sampé dön nam malü kün༔
Grant your blessings that everything we wish for༔
chö zhin drupar jingyi lop༔
Be accomplished in harmony with the Dharma!༔

<small>With that, the desired aims are requested. </small>

<small>Next, present the specific offering three times to the Noble Lady and recite the praises</small>

rangnyi kechik dren dzok su༔
Perfected in an instant of recollection,༔
jetsün pakmé kur salwé༔
I am vividly present in the form of the Noble Lady.༔
tukar da teng tam jangu༔
Upon the moon in my heart center,༔
dé lé özer pakyé trö༔
Boundless rays of light stream forth from the green tam,༔

pakma namtrül nyerchik dang༔
Inviting the Noble Lady’s twenty-one emanations༔
könchok gyamtso trin trowa༔
And an ocean of Precious Ones, swirling and billowing,༔
dün gyi namkhar chendrang gyur༔
Inviting them to appear in the sky before me.༔

tam, kyemé ösal chökü ying༔
Tam. From the non-arising space of luminous dharmakaya,༔
gakmé pakmé yeshe kur༔
In the unobstructed wisdom form of the Noble Lady༔
gang dül gyutrül drawar shar༔
Manifest as the Magical Net to touch any who are in need,༔
tsur shek arya taré dza༔
Please approach, arya Tare Jah!༔

dak dang dro la tuk tsé chir༔
Because of your love for me and all sentient beings,༔
nyi kyi dzutrül tu yi ni༔
And because you possess such miraculous powers,༔
jisi chöpa dak gyipa༔
For as long as I may honor you,༔
desi chomden zhuk su sol༔
Blessed One, please remain seated!༔

pema kamala ya stam༔

om, ngöjor yi lé jungwa yi༔
Om. Actually present and mentally created,༔
küntuzangpö chötrin gyi༔
These offering clouds of Samantabhadra,༔
chöying gyepar kang jé té༔
Lavishly filling the expanse of dharmadhatu—༔
pakma drolma khor ché chö༔
I offer to Noble Tara and her retinue!༔

om arya taré sapariwara benza argham padam pushpé düpé aloké gendhé naividyé shapda pratitsa soha

<small>With this, invite the deities and make offerings.</small>

om ah hung, kham sum nöchü paljor dang
Om ah Hung.༔ The three realms, environment and inhabitants, all richness and splendor,༔
dak lü longchö gé tsok kün
My body, possessions, and each and every virtue,༔
tukjé daknyi nam la bul
I offer to the lords of compassion.༔
zhé né jingyi lap tu sol
Accept them all and bestow your blessings, I pray!༔

om sarva tathagata ratna mandala pudza ho

<small>With that, make the specific mandala offering and recite the King of Praises</small>

om, jétsünma pakma drolma la chaktsel lo
Om. Homage to the noble lady Tara!

chaktsel taré nyurma pamo
Homage to Tara, swift and gallant;
tuttara yi jikpa selma
Homage to Tuttara, banishing fear;
turé dönkün jinpé drolma
Homage to Turé, all needs fulfilling;
sahé yigé khyö la düdo
With svaha we offer you honor and praise!

chaktsel drolma nyurma pamo
Homage to Tara, swift and gallant,
chen ni kéchik lok dang drama
With glance that flashes like flares of lightning;
jikten sumgön chukyé zhelgyi
Born on the heart of a blossoming lotus
gesar jewa lé ni jungma
That rose from the tears of the Triple-World’s Lord.

chaktsel tönké dawa küntu
Homage to you, your countenance glowing
gangwa gyani tsekpé zhelma
Like a hundred full moons gathered in autumn,
karma tong trak tsokpa namkyi
Lustrous and smiling with brilliant radiance,
raptu chewé örap barma
Like a thousand stars clustered, ablaze.

chaktsel serngo chuné kyé kyi
Homage, golden lady, your lotus-hand
pemé chak ni nampar gyenma
Is graced with a water flower, blue in color;
jinpa tsöndrü katup zhiwa
You are generosity, perseverance, diligence,
zöpa samten chöyül nyima
Serenity, patience and meditation.

chaktsel dézhin shekpé tsuktor
Homage to you, whose victories are endless,
tayé nampar gyelwa chöma
The jewel on the great Tathagata’s crown;
malü paröl chinpa topé

gyelwé sékyi shintu tenma

Those wanting children will have them,

döpa tamché topar gyur la

jetsün chomden dema tükjé chen
Noble and compassionate Tara, we pray to you:
dak dang tayé semchen tamché kyi
May we beings, all of us, infinite in number,
drip nyi jang zhing tsok nyi nyur dzok né
May we purify the two obscurations, swiftly perfect the two accumulations,
dzokpé sangyé topar dzedu söl
And so attain the state of perfect awakening!

dé matop kyi tserap kün tu yang
Until then, throughout all of our lives,
lha dang miyi dewa chok top né

tamché khyenpa drüpar chepa la

barchö dön gek rim dang né la sok

dümin chiwar gyurpa natsok dang
All that brings untimely death,
milam ngen dang tsenma ngenpa dang

The eight dangers and all harm—
nyurdu zhi zhing mepar dzedu söl

jikten jikten leni depa yi

Both worldly and transcendent,
pel zhing gyepé dön nam malüpa
Flourish and expand, and may all our wishes
bemé lhün gyi drüpar dzedu söl

drup la tsön zhing damchö pelwa dang

tak tu khyé drup zhel chok tongwa dang

tongnyi dön tok jangsem rinpoché

gyelwé kyilkhor zang zhing gawa der

nangwa tayé gyelwé ngönsum du

lungtenpa yang dak gi der top shok

daki tserap ngöné drüpé lha

khyé kyi tsen chok zangpo chidrawa

dendra khonar dak sok gyurwar shok

khyé la tö ching sölwa tapé tü

dak sok gang na nepé sa chok su

né dön ül pong tap tsö zhiwa dang

## The second: daily practice

<small>Second, the daily practice. It is good if a support of the Noble Lady is available; if not, visualize it mentally. Arrange the mandala and offerings.</small>

dün gyi namkhar jetsünma ngönsum du zhukpar gyur
In the sky before me, Noble Tara is present in actuality.

namo, könchok kün ngö jetsünmar
Namo. In the Noble Lady, embodiment of all the Three Jewels,
dak sok dro kün kyap su chi
I and all beings take refuge.
jangchup mönpé semkyé né
Arousing bodhicitta in aspiration,
zapmö lam la jukpar gyi
I will enter the profound path!

<small>To gather the accumulations, recite:</small>

jetsün pakma drolma dang
To the noble Lady Tara,
chok chu dü sum zhukpa yi
And to all the conquerors and their heirs
gyalwa sé ché tamché la
Who reside in the ten directions and three times,
küné dangwé chak gyi o
Sincerely and with delight, I pay homage.

metok dukpö marmé dri
I offer flowers and incense, lamps and perfume,
zhalzé rolmo lasokpa
Food, music and the like,
ngöjor yi kyi trül né bül
Both materially present and mentally created.
pakmé tsok nam zhé su sol
May they be accepted by the Noble Lady’s assembly!

tokma mé né danté bar
Throughout beginningless time until this very day,
mi gé chu dang tsamé nga
I have committed the ten non-virtues and the five without interval.
sem ni nyönmong wang gyurpé
I apologize for each and every evil deed committed
dikpa tamché shakpar gyi
Under the influence of disturbed states of mind.

nyentö rangyal jangchup sem
I rejoice in all merit and goodness
soso kyewo lasokpé
Created throughout the three times
dü sum gewa chi sakpé
By shravakas, pratyeka-buddhas and bodhisattvas,
sönam la ni dak yi rang
By ordinary beings, and all others.

semchen nam kyi sampa dang
In accordance with the capabilities
lo yi jedrak jitawar
And individual aspirations of sentient beings,
chechung tünmong tekpa yi
I ask you to turn the wheel of Dharma
chö kyi khorlo kor du sol
Of the general, greater and lesser vehicles.

khorwa jisi matong bar
Until samsara has been emptied,
nya ngen mi da tukjé yi
Do not pass into nirvana, but please, in your compassion,
dukngal gyamtsor jingwa yi
Care for each and every being
semchen nam la zik su sol
Sinking in the ocean of suffering.

dak gi sönam chi sakpa
May whatever merit I have gathered
tamché jangchup gyur gyur né
Become a cause for enlightenment,
ringpor mi tok drowa yi
And may I, in the near future,
drenpé pal du dak gyur chik
Become a splendid guide for sentient beings.

tsur shek arya taré dza༔

Locally found or transported from elsewhere,
zöpa dang ni tungpa nyi kyang

dön dang rim dang duk gi zirwé

döpa tamché topar gyur la
One’s every wish will be fulfilled,

## The Tambura of Devotion: A Supplication Invokingthe Crucial Essence of the Vows of the Noble Lady Wish-Fulfilling Wheel

chenrezikwangnyingjéchuterlé
From the vast lake of Avalokiteśvara’s compassion,
pendékundagyepépaltrungpa
Awater lily of benefit and happiness bloomed gloriously:
pagmadrölmadütsiönangchen
Noble Tārā, shining with the light of nectar—
jikchémünpé lab di zhiwardzö
Dispel the terrifying forces of darkness!

gyalkünyumgyurdungwaselwémen
Mother of all buddhas, balm that heals my pain,
jampémamakyegünyenchigpu
Loving nurse, only friend to all beings,
tserabtrengwarpül du machipé
In all my lifetimes, you have been my most sublime protector.
kyabkhyönyingpö bar du kyabsuten
Until the heart of awakening,I rely on you for refuge!

daksoklékyinarwédrowanam
Beings like me, tormented by karma,
gang du nékyangnétsönmugésok
Wherever we are born, are constantly plagued
mi döduméyangyangtsélak na
By illness, war, famine, and so on.
dugngaldamnédrongshikjetsünma
Drag me from this swamp of suffering, O Noble Lady!

bagchakjedrangnamtokchunyertar
Like ripples on water, my thoughts follow habitual patterns,
kéchikrererngendrögyu mang sak
And every single moment I create
khorwésaböndzé du mepa di
Many causes for lower rebirths.
zhitsadantachöchikjetsünma
Eradicate these endless seeds of saṃsāra right away, O Noble Lady!

dégyutsogchenngön du masongwé
Since thus far I have failed to gather the great accumulations,
depégyalwéten gor zhuklomkyang
Although I pride myself on having faithfully entered the Buddhadharma,
könchoksum la lotémakhelwé
I am unable to entrust myself to the Three Jewels.
ledribtsénéchungzhikjetsünma
Rip out these karmic obscurations from their root, O Noble Lady!

ngönsum du yangminpargyurtong na
Even if I see it ripen right before my eyes,
dadunglédanglékyidrebu la
I still don’t believe in the karmic law of cause and effect.
yichémakyéchenzenngurmikdok
I’m just a wild beast clad in saffron robes.
dakyigé la drongshikjetsünma
Lead my mindto virtue, O Noble Lady!

nyintsenkéchikmitak mi yilü
Day and night, moment by moment, my body decays.
yangdakjor la dönchenmadrub bar
Until I have fulfilled the great purpose of this excellent human life,
dogparkawachidakzhagpégyün
Cut the lasso of the Lord of Death, so difficult to escape,
yeshéraldrichöchikjetsünma
With your sword of wisdom, O Noble Lady!

nekabsuyangdön dang rimduktsön
In every situation, quickly protect mefrom the eight or sixteen dangers—
loburnyi du shingenchiwa dang
Evil forces, epidemics, poison, weapons,
méchuchensokgyédangchudrukgi
Sudden death at the hands of wicked people,
jiklényur tu kyobshikjetsünma
Fires, floods, or wild animals, O Noble Lady!

dagzinngengomsemkyigyünjisi
As long as self-clinging and bad habits are present in my mindstream,
desidugngalchuwotakbabpé
The river of suffering will continue to flow.
pungsokrangzhinyongsushepayi
Thus, I should know the nature of the skandhas and so on.
togpédrönméporzhikjetsünma
Light the lamp of that understanding for me, O Noble Lady!

dronammarshéjamdangnyingtsewa
Recognition of beings as my mothers, love, and compassion
tsiktsam min par zhengyidugngal dang
Are qualities I should not just talk about;
dakdéngösujewépanamkyi
Heroic bodhisattvas exchange their happiness for others’ suffering.
nyingtob lab chenkyéchikjetsünma
Help me to muster suchvast courage, O Noble Lady!

tserabkün tu chinchima log pé
In all my future lives, may I meet an authentic teacher
gewéshéjaldétenchöküntok
And fully understand all teachings they impart.
jangchub bar du tsé dang tarsok la
Destroy Māra’s army of obstacles to life and liberation
barchöddüpungzhomshikjetsünma
Until I reach awakening, O Noble Lady!

lomikdüldralzabmösherab den
Grant me the profound wisdom to see with pure eyes,
nyönmongbakkyichungwézozangwé
A good character with little inclination to afflictive mental states,
lab la rabgüdren dang shezhin par
Which fosters deep devotion to spiritual training with mindfulness and alertness.
dakgyüchödangséshikjetsünma
Mergemy mind with the Dharma, O Noble Lady!

namzhiktséyipenpadzogpétsé
When the karma that keeps me in this life is spent,
néchödmepardushéchutokné
Ease the agonies of death and help me realize the eleven thoughts;
kyegaktadralyingsu la dawé
Show me mytrue nature, luminosity, with decisive recognition of the dhātu,
ösal rang ngotröchikjetsünma
Free from the extremes of origination and cessation, O Noble Lady!

bardokéchikyütsamshar na yang
Even if the bardo arises for just a fleeting instant,
dagnangkyilkhorkhorlötro du dzok
Let my pure perception ofthe emanation and dissolution of the maṇḍala be complete.
ngöngadedenpemokö la sok
Take me to Abhirati, the lotus-arrayed realm of Sukhāvatī,
dagpézhing du kyölzhikjetsünma
Or any other buddha field—O Noble Lady!

samzhinsiparjugpamatogpa
Unless I consciously decide to be born in saṃsāra,
wangmélékyikhorwar mi khorwar
Don’t let me circle helplessly here due to my karma.
tsé ö tayegyalwézhabdrung du
Guide me toBuddha Boundless Life and Light
lung tön lamnadrongshikjetsünma
To receive his prophecy, O Noble Lady!

sangyéjön dang majönzhinggyatso
In the vast ocean of realms,whether there are buddhas or not,
tayékün tu damchösoktarzung
May I guard the sublime Dharma with my life
jangchubchöchokdzogpar tön gyipé
And fully teach the excellent conduct of the bodhisattvas.
mönlamdrubpardzöchikjetsünma
Please fulfill this wish of mine, O Noble Lady!

togménésakdigdribnyetungkün
While in this body, may all the misdeeds, karmic obscurations, and transgressions
lüten di la jangzhingdagpa dang
I have accumulated since time immemorial be purified,
desichötünpaldangchimékyi
And until then, let me be in harmony with the Dharma,
tsewangngödrubtsölzhikjetsünma
And grant the siddhi of immortality, O Noble Lady!

milam du yangjangchubchokgisem
Even in my dreams, may bodhicitta never wane,
mi nyamdükyilékünyongpangté
And may I fully turn away from Māra’s machinations.
gosumjawachigyitendroyi
Let all my actions of body, speech, and mind be a powerful source
pendézungsupelzhikjetsünma
Of benefit and prosperity for the teachings and beings, O Noble Lady!

chögyétsé di namyengrabpangné
Let me leave behind the eight worldly dharmas and distractions of this life,
beténaksubarchödmachipar
So that in the solitude of the woods, unbothered by obstacles,
drub la nyingpordrilwétsédiru
I may concentrate on meditation practicealone.
milüdöndendzöchikjetsünma
In this way, make this human life meaningful, O Noble Lady!

dakgidantarnyongwédukha di
Let this duḥkha I’m experiencing right now
drowaküngyidugngalgyudangdré
Quickly exhaust the causes and effects
nyur tu zénéchinchédakzhenkün
Of all living beings’ sufferings,
dénédewartrichikjetsünma
And guide us from happiness to happiness, O Noble Lady!

pendéjungnégyalwétenpachok
Let the supreme Buddhadharma, source of benefit and happiness,
nubméchokchukün tu dar zhinggyé
Spread and flourish in all ten directions without ever waning,
dédzinkyechendampamalüpé
And please prolong the lives of all the great masters
kutse ring du sing shikjetsünma
Who upholdit, O Noble Lady!

khyeparngedönnyingpozabmödön
Especially, grant all beings the good fortune to hear
drokünnawarbabpékalzang den
The profound truth of the definitive essence,
dé la gawélhalunöjintsok
And lift up with praise to heavenly heights
upanggung du töchikjetsünma
The hosts of devas, nāgas, and yakṣaswell disposed to it, O Noble Lady!

gyalkhamkün tu mi démingméching
In all countries, let the word “unhappiness” never be heard again,
tsesöpalgyéten la güpasok
And from the vast jewel cloud of a golden age of happiness and joy,
dzokdendekyinorbütrinchenlé
Filled with longevity, merit, prosperity, and devotion to the teachings,
dögücharpapobchikjetsünma
Let a rain of all I could possibly wish for fall, O Noble Lady!

gyalwétendangsemchendekyi la
Burn the thicket of misguided thoughts and deeds
nampartsewé mi dangmimayin
Of all humans and non-humans who inflict harm
samjorlogparzhugpétsangtsingkün
Upon the Buddhadharma and happiness of beings
tutobmechésekshikjetsünma
In the fires of your power, O Noble Lady!

khanyammanamdüminchisokkyi
Free my mothers, infinite in number, from the fear of untimely death,
jikdralgéchüpal la nganggichö
And let them spontaneously enjoy the glory of the ten virtues.
könchoklabpézhirdroktarpayi
Grounded in the Three Jewels and the trainings,
khalogyen du gyurzhikjetsünma
Steer their course upward to liberation, O Noble Lady!

khyepardak dang dak la töchénam
Especially, don’t let me and those who depend on me
namkünbarchéwang du mi drowar
Ever fall under the sway of obstacles,
tsédangdrubpatarchintaktengyi
And plant the pillar of long and stable life
sokgikawatsukshikjetsünma
So that our lives and practice reach completion, O Noble Lady!

di chikün tu lotéchepélha
Goddess to whom I entrust my heart in this and all my future lives,
düsumgyalwéyumgyurchomdenmé
Bhagavatī, mother of all buddhas of the three times,
mönpédrebudrubparmadzé na
If you do not fulfill my aspirations,
kyabzhendak la mé do jetsünma
No one else can help me, O Noble Lady!

togmédünétsoknyidrubpé tob
You attained the inconceivable body of space
sam gyi mi khyabnamkhékunyepé
Through the power of the two accumulations gathered throughout beginningless time,
tséchikdungwésölwadeblak na
So when I supplicate you with one-pointed, fervent yearning,
jinlabtugjémachungjetsünma
Let your blessings and compassion be unconstrained, O Noble Lady!

chukyé ü na töndagyepéku
At the center of a lotus, your body shines like the full autumn moon,
dorjédewélangtsoshintudzok
Flush with the youthful vigor of vajra bliss.
drimé ö kyikyilkhortalepé
With the boundless halo ofyour stainless light,
lézhitrinlétsölzhikjetsünma
Grant the four enlightened activities, O Noble Lady!

gang la regpéchönordö dang tar
With your palm, formed from a hundred merits,
sam guyizhinjowéchogjingyi
Which grants the supreme gift of anything one could wish for—
chagtilsönamgyalédrubpadé
Dharma, wealth, sense pleasures, and liberation—to whomever it touches,
dakgichiworjukshikjetsünma
Caress my head, O Noble Lady!

tebsin bar du rinchendongbütser
From the exquisite, freshly opened lotus
yiongpemasar du zhepalé
Atop the precious stem between your thumb and ring finger
choktündrangtsichiyangjowadé
Drips the wish-granting honey of supreme and common siddhis.
dakyisimpakyéchikjetsünma
Let it fill my mind with bliss, O Noble Lady!

mutikdrachésergyidrilchungchen
Your magical appearance is graceful and charming, adorned with strings of pearls,
lhadzégyensokrölgeknampartrül
Small golden bells, divine garments, and ornaments,
charkhéserzhünzijitongbarwa
Ablaze with the thousandfold splendor of the molten gold of sunrise.
depémikngar tön chikjetsünma
Reveal yourself before my faithful eyes, O Noble Lady!

rabjamzhing na ngalsojinpépül
Sublime giver of rest in infinite pure realms,
zagmédawagyepégyabyölchen
With the stainless full moon behind you,
mi shikdorjékyilmotrungchepa
Seated in indestructible vajra posture,
chitsukgyen du zhukshikjetsünma
Adorn the crown of my head, O Noble Lady!

chösungtsangpéyangkyizabpa dang
With your Brahmā voice, please teach
gyachégyün mi ché tön dribsumgyi
The profound and vast Dharma without cease,
drawakünchékünkyangjangchubkyi
Cut the web of the three obscurations,
gyalsarugyungtsölzhikjetsünma
And relieve us at the victorious level of awakening, O Noble Lady!

dor na kyabkyinyingpokhyöyinpé
In short, since you are my essential refuge,
jangchubma tob bar du tagpardak
Until I reach awakening,
barchödükyiwang du mi tangwar
Never let me fall under the sway of the obstacle-maker Māra,
dönnyitaruchinpékadrintsöl
And grant your grace so I bring the twofold benefit to completion!

From the single beryl lute of your secret speech,
yang kyi yenlak gyatsö rölmo ni
Music from the ocean of melodious sounds
chöpung jewé dang su yong sharwa
Appears to all in a melody of a million gathered teachings—
pagma yizhin khorlor chagtsal lo
O Noble Lady, Wish-Fulfilling Wheel, to you I pay homage.

gang tuk nyi su mepé yeshé ying
Your mind is the non-dual union of wisdom and basic space,
zungdzin tröpé juk dang tül gyur kyang
In which any stirring of the constructs of grasper and grasped has been stilled,
rik dang tsewé ziji tayé pa
And yet the majesty of your knowledge and love is infinite—
pagma yizhin khorlor chagtsal lo
O Noble Lady, Wish-Fulfilling Wheel, to you I pay homage.

dral dang nampar minpa mizepé
Sublime mother, in your mesmerizing display
yönten drugchu tsa zhi gek changwé
Of the sixty-four inexhaustible qualities of freedom and ripening,
sé ché gyal kün tenpa trogpé yum
You unsteady even the victors and their heirs—
pagma yizhin khorlor chagtsal lo
O Noble Lady, Wish-Fulfilling Wheel, to you I pay homage.

gyün gyi tak ching kha tar khyabpa dang
In a continuous stream, pervading all of space,
lhündrub du mi dzepé trinlé kyi
Your spontaneous and effortless activity,
düljé rewa kong dzé pagsam wang
Like a wish-granting tree, answers the hopes of those to be tamed—
pagma yizhin khorlor chagtsal lo
O Noble Lady, Wish-Fulfilling Wheel, to you I pay homage.

dorna tob chu ngawa tamché kyi
In short, to fully accomplish the sworn resolve
tugkyé damcha yongsu dzogpé chir
Of all those who enjoy mastery over the ten powers,
dro dül dzepa sam gyi mi khyabpé
You perform unimaginable deeds to tame living beings—
pagma yizhin khorlor chagtsal lo
O Noble Lady, Wish-Fulfilling Wheel, to you I pay homage.

detar güpé chagtsal söldeb na
Thus, in devotion I pay homage and supplicate you—
yeshé zigpé dzok den trinchen lé
Grant your blessings so that your wisdom insight, like the great clouds of the perfect age,
ngödrub dögü druchar rab yowé
Showers down a gentle rain of all desired siddhis,
pendé gatön gyepar jin gyi lob
Spreading an abundant feast of joy and benefit.

<small>For the sake of Gyurme Namdak, one whose expansive vision is rich with insight, Jamyang Khyentse Wangpo composed an auspicious number of verses in homage and supplication to the Noble Lady, Moon of Compassion. </small>

## Words of Auspiciousness to Accomplish One’s Aims

Namastāraye!

khyö tsen tö pa tsam gyi kyang
By merely hearing your name,
dro nam khor tso lé dröl wé
Beings are freed from saṃsāra’s ocean.
ngo tsar kho nar yi chak pé
Enraptured by this wondrous miracle,
tsé chik gü pé söl deb na
I supplicate you with total devotion.

rab dzé ma gé jé wé dül
Your gorgeous form is adorned with virtuous marks,
chik tu pung pé gé tsen chen
Like millions of powdered emeralds piled up high.
dzé ku dak gi mik lam du
May you display this splendid form before my eyes,
zhi dzum nyam gyé röl par dzö
With a hundred peacefully smiling expressions.

jik ten jik ten dé pa yi
From the accomplishment of your secret vidyā mantra,
ngö drub rin chen gya tsö yik
An ocean of precious treasures,
rik pé sang ngak drub pa lé
Siddhis, both mundane and supramundane, well forth.
sa sum pen dé jin gyur chik
Thus, may you grant benefit and bliss throughout the three planes.

zab ching chok tu zhi wé ying
Basic space is profound and sublime peace.
chok dang cha lé nam dröl wé
It is free of directions and parts.
jin gyi lab pé ké chik la
When bestowing your blessings, at that very moment,
zung dzin u mar tim dzé söl
Make duality dissolve into the central channel!

khyé par nyik lé cher nyik pé
Most especially, for all of us living in dread of the decline
dü kyi gü pé rab trak nam
Of these dark and degenerate times,
nyin tsen mé par gyal wé yum
Mother of All Victors, day and night I turn to you.
khyö lé kyab zhen sem pa dral
I know no other protection than you.

dé lé tuk jé chak kyang né
So, extend your hand of compassion,
jik pa ta dak sal wa dang
Dispel all my fears,
kün gé né su uk yung wé
And grant me the bountiful fortune
kal pa zang po drub par dzö
Of finding relief in a perfect realm.

lar la né kab dé zhi yi
In the meantime, I beseech you, make the qualities of the four perfections
yön ten mé tok dzé bar war
Blossom with blazing beauty,
tar tuk ku sum mi ché pé
And laden with such abundance,
zung juk nyé ma dü dzé söl
The indivisible three kāyas are ultimately unified.

dor na di né jang chub bar
In short, from now until awakening,
pak ma gyé pé chen zur dang
May I remain forever
nam yang mi dral drok pa lé
Under Noble Tārā’s joyful gaze,
dö gü ré kha dzok gyur chik
And thus may the beautiful picture of all desired aims be perfected.

<small>These small flowers of auspicious supplication were scattered by the vidyādhara Mañjughoṣa to grant some relief to those living in dread of the decline of these dark times. Grant your blessings that these aspirations be fulfilled! Sarvadā śreyo bhavatu.</small>

## The Sage's Powerful Words of Truth: A Prayer for the Spread of the Omniscient Buddha's Teachings

lama dang sangye changchub sempa thamché la chagtsal lo
Homage to the guru and all the buddhas and bodhisattvas!

kalzang sangye tong lé khyepardu
Among the thousand buddhas of this fortunate age,
ngotsar u dum wa ra tar pak shing
You are the most wondrous and majestic, as rare as an _uḍumbara_ flower,
tsöpé dü dir zhingkham yong zungwa
You who care for our world during this age of conflict,
tub chok dön kün drub la chaktsal lo
Mighty sage, Siddhārtha, to you I pay homage!

chok chü sangye changsem nyenrang dang
All you buddhas, bodhisattvas, śrāvakas and pratyekabuddhas in all directions,
jamyang chenzik chak na dorjé dzin
Great bodhisattvas, Mañjuśrī, Avalokiteśvara,
jampa lasok changchub sempa dang
Vajrapāṇi, Maitreya and the rest,
neten chudruk té rab chenpo dün
Together with the sixteen sthaviras and seven great patriarchs,

dzamling gyen chok mejung lobpön ché
Six ornaments and two supreme ones of this world, the two marvellous ācāryas,
naljor wangchuk gyechu tsa zhi sok
Eighty-four mahāsiddhas, and the rest,
khyepar kadrin nyammé pema jung
And especially, the Lotus-born Guru, whose kindness is beyond compare,
zhitso trisong vairo atisha
Śāntarakṣita, Tri Songdetsen, Vairotsana and Atiśa,

gongma nam nga mar mi dakpo jé
Five Sakya Patriarchs, Marpa, Milarepa and Gampopa,
jamyang lama lasok gya bö kyi
Lama Tsongkhapa, Mañjughoṣa in person, and all the rest,
khé dang drubpé rigdzin tsok nam dang
All you great learned and accomplished vidyādharas of India and Tibet,
yidam zhitro khandro chökyong sok
And all you peaceful and wrathful _yidam_ deities, ḍākinīs and dharmapālas—
tukdam né né kul lo tukjé gong
We invoke you! Turn your wisdom minds towards us, and look upon us with compassion!

tubten dulwa dodé ngönpa dang
All the teachings of the Buddha—Vinaya, Sūtra, Abhidharma,
sang ngak gyüdé chenpo nampa zhi
The four classes of tantra within the Secret Mantra,
gangjong shé gyü ka chen chupo dang
The teachings of the ten great pillars of the study lineage in the Land of Snows,
drubgyü shingta ché gyé lasokpa
And the eight great chariots of the practice lineage,
tubten do dang ngak kyi tsul tadak
All the sūtra and mantra traditions of the Buddha’s teachings—
shintu nyampar gyurpa di la zik
Look now and see the extent to which they have all declined!

ngejung tsultrim ngurmik gyaltsen tok
The saffron-robed saṅgha, upholders of the victory banner of discipline and renunciation,
zhenpen zolmé changchub sempa dang
Bodhisattvas who, without pretense or deception, genuinely seek to benefit others,
damtsik den zhing kyedzok rik ngak chang
And holders of awareness mantras who maintain the commitments, and practise generation and completion—
tsennyi denpa rimö marmé tar
Look how these practitioners have been reduced to pale imitations of the real thing, like a mere drawing of a butter lamp!
gyurpa di la yeshe chenden nam
Look upon us now with your eyes of wisdom, and care for us in your compassion, we pray!
ngepar gong la tukjé kyab tu sol
Beings of this evil age with its five degenerations,

dü ngen nyikma nga dö semchen nam
Are caught in the bonds of turbulent and destructive emotions, their cravings and desires.
nyönmong tob drak dö sé chingwé ching
The followers of the teachings fight amongst themselves,
ten la zhuk nam trakdok lungmar gyi
Spurred on by the fierce winds of jealousy,
tenpa nang truk tawé nyikma gyé
And degenerate views are widespread.

tsöpachen du gyurpé ten sel gyi
Through these defilements, brought about by conflicts within the tradition,
drib kyi chökyong karchok sungma yel
The Dharma protectors, guardians of all that is positive, have turned away from us,
gyalkham küntu lhamin lalö kheng
While warrior-like _asuras_ and barbarians fill the world—
di dang didrar gyurpé tenpa nub
In these and other ways, the teachings have declined.

kyema kyi hü gönpo tukjé chen
_Kyema Kyihü_! O compassionate protectors!
khyö nam tukdam tutsal tob kyé la
Increase the power and strength of your sacred commitments!
dü ngen kyowé zhumpa chi la nga
Why would you be discouraged by the sadness of these evil times?

tob chü wangchuk wang chu ngawé na
Since you are masters of the ten strengths and possessors of the ten powers,
tenpa nyampa didak sorchü ching
You must surely heal and restore the teachings.
khé tsün drubtob tsemar gyurpa ni
Send us genuine teachers who are learned, disciplined and accomplished,
mangpor trul né tenpa sowar dzö
To breathe fresh life into the Buddhadharma!

pak bö nyi su dzokden chima yi
In both India and Tibet, may the precious lamps of joyful celebration,
gatön rinchen drönmé barwa dang
Be set ablaze, to usher in another golden age,
gyalten rimé petsal dar la bab
And may the non-sectarian, lotus-like teachings of the Buddha, flourish and blossom.

dé la khuwé tsok kyi sem gyur zhing
May the minds of those who harbour ill-will towards the teachings be changed,
kün kyang ten la dün dang gé chur chö
And filled with inspiration to practise the ten virtuous actions.
tendzin gendün dé yi dzamling khyab
May the saṅgha, the holders of the teachings, fill this world of ours,
shé dang drubpa dar zhing gyepar shok
And study and practice increase and spread.

tsuklakhang nam chöpé trin tsok gyé
May vast clouds of offerings fill all monasteries and temples,
dampé chö kyi nga chen rabtu drok
And the great drum of the Dharma resound far and wide,
mutek langpö lepa gem jé ching
Annihilating the deluded arguments of the _tīrthikas_,
tubten chok lé nampar gyal gyur chik
And sounding the victory of the Buddhadharma in all directions.

tendzin kyé nam zhabpé tenpa dang
May the lives of all the holders of the teachings be secure,
tenpé jindak tsé dang sönam pel
And may the lives and merits of benefactors increase,
ten la nö nam ming gi lhakmar gyur
May even the names of those who would harm the teachings disappear,
tenpé gyaltsen chok chur dreng gyur chik
And may the victory banner of the teachings be raised in all directions!

<small>Chökyi Lodrö wrote down whatever came to mind at the foot of the bodhi tree in Vajrāsana (Bodhgayā) in the noble land of India. May these words be blessed so that they all come true. Sarvadā maṅgalaṃ! Translated by Adam Pearcey, 2007.</small>

## Tea offering prayers

lha dang lhamin chöpen gyi
Devas and asuras,with their crowned heads,
zhab kyi pemo la tü dé
Bow to your lotus feet.
pongpa kün lé dröl dzé ma
You liberate from every form of destitution.
drölma yum la chöpa bül
To you, Mother Tārā, I make this offering.

dak gi tserab ngön né drubpé lha
O goddess! Life after life, I have devoted my practice to you.
düsum sangyé kün gyi trinlé ma
You are the enlightened activity of all buddhas, past, present, and future;
ngojang zhal chik chak nyi nyur zhi pa
Turquoise colored, with one face and two hands, you are swift, serene, and courageous;
yum gyur utpal nam la chöpa bül
Mother of the buddhas, holder of the utpala flower, to you I make this offering.

zhalzé rogya den zhing yitrogpa
This exquisite food, with a hundred flavors,so perfectly prepared,
legjar di ni gyalwa sé ché la
I offer with devotion to the victors and their heirs.
depé pülwé drowa didak kün
By the merit of this offering, may all beings
jorden tingdzin zé la chöpar shok
Come to enjoy the nourishing food of samādhi.

## Prayers for conclusion

gang jong rimé tenpa gyatsö dak
Lord of the ocean of the non-sectarian teachings of the Land of Snows,
ngotsar kababdün gyi khorlo gyur
Wondrous one who turned the wheel of the seven transmissions,
jampal yang ngö khyentsé wangpo yi
Khyentse Wangpo, Manjushri in person—
ringluk tenpa dargyé trashi shok
May your tradition and teachings spread and flourish—may there be aus
piciousness!

chimé drubpé rikdzin bima lé
The deathless, accomplished vidyadhara Vimala’s
sang sum yeshé dorjé gyutrül gar
Magical dance of the wisdom vajra of the three secrets—
jampal yang ngö chökyi lodrö kyi
Chökyi Lodrö, Manjushri in person—
tenpa dar zhing gyépé trashi shok
May your teachings spread and increase—may there be auspiciousness!

<small>Thus, aspirations and auspicious verses such as these, connected with the occasion, should be widely extended as appropriate, for well-being.</small>

khyentsé wangpö yeshé gyutrül gar
Magical dance of the wisdom of Khyentse Wangpo,
chökyi lodrö sang sum trülpé ku
Chökyi Lodrö, emanation body of the three secrets,
jamyang tupten chökyi gyatso yi
Jamyang Thupten Chökyi Gyatso—
zhabpé tenching trinlé gyegyur chik
May your lotus feet remain firm and your activity increase!

dak ni kyewa tamché du
In all my lifetimes,
tenpé khurchen khurwar shok
May I carry the great burden of the teachings.
khurchen khurwar ma nü kyang
Even if I am unable to carry that great burden,
tenpa nyam kyi dokpa yi
The worry that the teachings may decline—
sem trel tsam yang jungwar shok
May at least a little of it weigh on my mind.
