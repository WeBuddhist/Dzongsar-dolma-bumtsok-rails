# Essence of the Two Accumulations: The Ritual for the Elaborate Outer Practice from the Mind Treasure, “Tārā’s Profound Essence ^0

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^I-0]]

## A fifteen-verse prayer to the King of the Śākyas ^I-0

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^I-1]]

In devotion I prostrate to the guru and Mañjughoṣa. ^I-1

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^I-2]]

When you sat under the great Bodhi Tree and the earth and sky were filled with hordes of māras,
Raining down cascades of vajras, spears, wheels,fire, and entire mountains and glaciers—
Magical displays unleashed by the mighty Flower-Arrowed One striving to avert your awakening—
You defeated them all with nothing but love in your heart and attained perfect buddhahood – to you I bow my head. ^I-2

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^I-3]]

When, for the benefit of others
And with perfect intention and application,
You gave rise to the noble resolve to attain supreme awakening,
Even the earth shook in six motions. ^I-3

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^I-4]]

You mastered generosity and discipline,
Cultivated forbearance and diligence,
And broughtmeditation and wisdom to their culmination—
Homage to you, in whomthe perfections were complete. ^I-4

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^I-5]]

Protector of beings, the moment you were born,
The learned and wise of this world were delighted,
Māras grew deeply concerned,
And all those who proclaimed mistaken paths were terrified. ^I-5

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^I-6]]

And so, the activity of your teaching
Shines forthlike the light of the sun;
And being never deceiving in any way,
You are the true friend in whomI take refuge. ^I-6

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^I-7]]

When your sublime teaching, like the lion’s roar,
Resounded throughout this world with its gods,
Brahmā and Viṣṇu were speechless,
And the teacher of the godsowered like a fox. ^I-7

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^I-8]]

Sages, like Akṣapāda and Vālmīki,
Seers, such as Vyāsa and Vāsiṣṭha,
And many others of great renown —
To you, who disarmed them all with the Dharma, I bow. ^I-8

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^I-9]]

Having attained supreme awakening,
You accomplished beings’ benefit in the most wondrous ways;
And for as long as you were not yet free from the thirst for existence,
You gave your body and life – this fills me with awe! ^I-9

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^I-10]]

Without regard for your own well-being
You proclaimed: “I will benefit you!”,
And so you hold us, who are without protection, in your care.
Protector, in you I take refuge. ^I-10

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^I-11]]

Victorious One, you tamed the hosts of māras,
Disarmed all those who proclaimed mistaken paths,
Liberated countless śrāvaka disciples,
And bestowed prophecies upon sublime bodhisattvas. ^I-11

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^I-12]]

Those who perceive such abundance of excellence
Strive to accomplish your sublime qualities;
And so I, who follow the path to awakening,
Once again take refuge in you. ^I-12

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^I-13]]

You who made Mañjughoṣa your principal heir,
Śāriputra your foremost disciple,
And Maitreya your sublime regent;
What wise person could ever doubt you! ^I-13

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^I-14]]

What need is there to speak of your sublime qualities,
When just to see those of your heirs is measure enough?
For similarly does the splendor of the breaking dawn
Announce the radiance of the lotus-opening sun. ^I-14

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^I-15]]

Having gained wisdom complete in every aspect—
Knowing the true nature of phenomena as well as their full extent—
All the enlightened deeds you had undertaken,
You accomplishedperfectly;to you I pay homage. ^I-15

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^I-16]]

Through this praise of the perfect buddha bhagavān,
The teacher of beings from the Śākya clan,
Born into the line of Ikṣvāku,
May all beings swiftly attain omniscience. ^I-16

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^I-17]]

<small>This prayer in fifteen stanzas to the Bhagavān King of Śākyas was composed by the glorious Sakya Paṇḍita at the Tsuklagkhang in Lhasa. Translated by Christian Bernert.</small> ^I-17

## A Garland of Utpala Blossoms: The Lineage Prayer for Zaptik Drolchok, the Mind Treasure of Tārā ^II-0

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^II-1]]

Buddha Samantabhadrī, dharmakāya’s Transcendent Wisdom,
Vajravārāhī, sambhogakāya’s total perfection;
Sublime mother Tara, nirmāṇakāya’s magical display;
To you I pray, grant me siddhis, common and supreme! ^II-1

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^II-2]]

Padmākara, our ‘second buddha’,
Master of the treasury of secret mantra, lord over a hundred thousand ḍākinīs,
Bodhisattva prince Yeshé Rolpa Tsal,
To you I pray, grant me siddhis, common and supreme! ^II-2

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^II-3]]

Chokgyur Dechen Lingpa, revealer of profound termas;
Dorjé Ziji Tsal, holder of the seven direct blessings;
Pema Garwang, elucidator of the buddhas’ teachings;
To you I pray, grant me siddhis, common and supreme! ^II-3

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^II-4]]

<small>Addition</small> ^II-4

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^II-5]]

beró namtrul gyurmé tsewang tsal
kunzig jamyang chökyi lodö dang
mindrol tsolzé khyabdak manga lar
To you I pray, grant me siddhis, common and supreme! ^II-5

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^II-6]]

<small>Composed by Mangala</small> ^II-6

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^II-7]]

Holders of the treasury of ripening and liberation, vast and profound,
Root and lineage gurus, deities of the Sublime Lady, swift to act,
Infinite gurus, devas and ḍākinīs, with Vajra Dharma protectors,
To you I pray, grant me siddhis, common and supreme! ^II-7

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^II-8]]

Let the earth of my mind be soaked in renunciation,
Letting the green shoots of pure altruistic intention sprout and grow,
Their branches laden with the fruits of great primordial wisdom,
That are the benefit of self and others, spontaneously fulfilled! ^II-8

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^II-9]]

## The Essence of the Two Accumulations: The Ritual for the Elaborate Outer Practice From the mind Treasure cycle The Profound Essence of Tara ^1-0

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-1]]

Namo Gurvya aryatarayai! ^1-1

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-2]]

<small>In honor of Tara, mother to all the guides</small>
<small>And their heirs in all three times—</small>
<small>She who has wonderful resolve and activity—</small>
<small>I set out here the practice according to Kriya and Carya.</small> ^1-2

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-3]]

<small>Since this Profound Essence is a condensation of every quintessential profundity, it is not necessary to resort to much elaboration. Nevertheless there are two degrees of detail: one slightly elaborate and the other as a daily practice.</small> ^1-3

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-4]]

<small>For the first, in front of a painting or statue of the noble Lady Tara, or whatever image of the Three Jewels you can acquire, arrange, on a clean surface, the two waters, the general offerings, the round white torma, the cleansing water, the offering mandala, grain for tossing, and other articles. If you can, keep the conduct of bathing and cleanliness. If unable to do that, at least perform the practice when you have not consumed meat or alcohol, such as in the morning. With an attitude of renunciation and bodhicitta, recite these verses of refuge and bodhicitta:</small> ^1-4

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-5]]

In the sky before me, Noble Tara is present in actuality. ^1-5

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-6]]

Namo. In the Noble Lady, embodiment of all the Three Jewels,
I and all beings take refuge.
Arousing bodhicitta in aspiration,
I will enter the profound path! ^1-6

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-7]]

<small>To gather the accumulations, recite:</small> ^1-7

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-8]]

To the noble Lady Tara,
And to all the conquerors and their heirs
Who reside in the ten directions and three times,
Sincerely and with delight, I pay homage. ^1-8

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-9]]

I offer flowers and incense, lamps and perfume,
Food, music and the like,
Both materially present and mentally created.
May they be accepted by the Noble Lady’s assembly! ^1-9

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-10]]

Throughout beginningless time until this very day,
I have committed the ten non-virtues and the five without interval.
I apologize for each and every evil deed committed
Under the influence of disturbed states of mind. ^1-10

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-11]]

I rejoice in all merit and goodness
Created throughout the three times
By shravakas, pratyeka-buddhas and bodhisattvas,
By ordinary beings, and all others. ^1-11

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-12]]

In accordance with the capabilities
And individual aspirations of sentient beings,
I ask you to turn the wheel of Dharma
Of the general, greater and lesser vehicles. ^1-12

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-13]]

Until samsara has been emptied,
Do not pass into nirvana, but please, in your compassion,
Care for each and every being
Sinking in the ocean of suffering. ^1-13

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-14]]

May whatever merit I have gathered
Become a cause for enlightenment,
And may I, in the near future,
Become a splendid guide for sentient beings. ^1-14

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-15]]

<small>To bless the offerings:</small> ^1-15

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-16]]

om benza amrita kundali hana hana hung pé ^1-16

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-17]]

om sobhava shuddho sarva dharma sobhava shuddho hang ^1-17

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-18]]

<small>Thereby cleanse and purify.</small> ^1-18

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-19]]

Here, in the Realm of Lotus Array,
Perfectly formed and abundantly decorated
There are offerings surpassing thought—
Divine articles created from samadhi. ^1-19

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-20]]

om benza argham padam pushpé düpé aloké gendhé naividyé shapda ah hung ^1-20

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-21]]

om benza saparana kham ^1-21

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-22]]

<small>Following that, begin with honoring and making offerings to the Three Jewels in general:</small> ^1-22

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-23]]

The masters, the conquerors, and their heirs of the ten directions and four times are in the sky before me, vajra-samaja! ^1-23

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-24]]

Protector of all sentient beings without exception,
Deity who defeats the endless hordes of maras,
Since you are fully omniscient,
Blessed One and your retinue, please approach this place! ^1-24

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-25]]

pema kamala ya stam ^1-25

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-26]]

<small>Next recite the Seven Branch Prayer:</small> ^1-26

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-27]]

To all the buddhas, lions of men,
In all directions of the universe, through past and present and future:
To every single one of you, I bow in homage;
Devotion fills my body, speech, and heart. ^1-27

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-28]]

Through the power of this prayer, Aspiring to Good Action,
All the victorious ones appear, vivid before my mind,
And I multiply my body as many times as there are atoms in the universe,
Each one bowing in prostration to all the buddhas. ^1-28

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-29]]

In every atom preside as many buddhas as there are atoms,
And around them, all their bodhisattva heirs,
And so I imagine them filling completely
The entire space of reality. ^1-29

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-30]]

Saluting them with an endless ocean of praise,
An ocean of sounds of different melodies,
I sing of the buddhas’ noble qualities
And praise all those who have gone to perfect bliss. ^1-30

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-31]]

To every buddha, I make offerings:
Of the loveliest flowers, of beautiful garlands,
Of music and perfumed ointments, the best of parasols,
The brightest lamps, and finest incense. ^1-31

From the miseries of samsara’s lower realms,
From every peril, now and always! ^1-184

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-185]]

Increase our life, merit, capacity, experience and realization!
May harmful notions not intrude!
May the twofold awakened mind arise, free of effort!
May our aims be fulfilled in accordance with the Dharma! ^1-185

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-186]]

From now until supreme enlightenment,
Like a mother protects her only child,
Safeguard us always with your kindness!
Let us be indivisible from you! ^1-186

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-187]]

<small>With this extraordinary prayer for your desired aims, invoke the oath.</small> ^1-187

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-188]]

The front visualization melts into light and dissolves into me.
Blessed to be indivisible from her,
I turn into the form of Noble Tara,
Visible yet insubstantial. ^1-188

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-189]]

<small>While you keep in mind the vivid presence and pride of being the Noble Lady yourself, recite the ten syllable mantra as many times as you can.</small> ^1-189

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-190]]

om taré tuttaré turé soha ^1-190

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-191]]

<small>Then recite the hundred-syllable mantra.</small> ^1-191

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-192]]

om benza sato samaya manupalaya benza sato tenopa tishta dridho mé bhava sutohyo mé bhava supohyo mé bhava anurakto mé bhava sarva siddhi mé prayatsa sarva karma su tsa mé tsittam shri yang kuru hung ha ha ha ha ho bhagavan sarva tatagata benza ma mé muntsa benzi bhava maha samaya sato ah ^1-192

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-193]]

For anything lacking or incomplete,
Or beyond my ability to perform—
Whatever mistakes I have committed,
Please bear witness and forgive them all! ^1-193

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-194]]

Here, together with this physical support,
Please remain for as long as samsara endures,
And grant us health, long life, prosperity,
And all supreme accomplishments! ^1-194

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-195]]

om supratishta benza yé soha ^1-195

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-196]]

<small>Thus, scatter flowers and offer the request to remain.</small> ^1-196

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-197]]

<small>If there is no support,</small> ^1-197

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-198]]

Om. You, who act for the welfare of sentient beings
And bestow accomplishment accordingly—
Please depart for your buddhafields,
But return, again and again! ^1-198

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-199]]

benza muh ^1-199

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-200]]

<small>Thus, let the invited deities to depart.</small> ^1-200

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-201]]

As the deity is indivisible from my own mind,
I settle within the original state of dharmadhatu. ^1-201

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-202]]

<small>Thus, foster the view of the true nature.</small> ^1-202

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-203]]

Through this virtue may I quickly
Accomplish the state of Noble Tara.
And may I bring each and every being,
Without a single exception, to that very same state. ^1-203

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-204]]

<small>Dedicate the merit and make noble aspirations.</small> ^1-204

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-205]]

Like the wish-fulfilling jewel and the vase of bounty,
Unimpeded, every wish you fulfill.
Noble Tara, conquerors and your heirs,
Bestow the auspiciousness that we may be forever nurtured by your compassion. ^1-205

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-206]]

<small>With that, utter verses of auspiciousness and toss flowers.</small> ^1-206

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-207]]

Noble and compassionate Tara, we pray to you:
May we beings, all of us, infinite in number,
May we purify the two obscurations, swiftly perfect the two accumulations,
And so attain the state of perfect awakening! ^1-207

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-208]]

Until then, throughout all of our lives,
May we find supreme happiness among gods and humans!
May all obstacles to the attainment of omniscience,
All harmful forces, all sickness and disease, ^1-208

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-209]]

All that brings untimely death,
All bad dreams and evil omens,
The eight dangers and all harm—
May all be swiftly pacified and purged! ^1-209

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-210]]

May auspiciousness, happiness, and prosperity,
Both worldly and transcendent,
Flourish and expand, and may all our wishes
Be effortlessly and spontaneously fulfilled! ^1-210

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-211]]

May we endeavor in the practice, may the Dharma spread,
May we always meditate on you, and behold your perfect face;
May we realize the nature of emptiness, and may precious bodhicitta
Develop and expand, just like the waxing moon! ^1-211

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-212]]

May I be reborn in a beautiful lotus flower,
In that excellent and joyous buddha-realm,
And may the Buddha Amitabha himself
Grant me the prophecy foretelling my enlightenment! ^1-212

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-213]]

Divine goddess whom I’ve accomplished in previous lives,
Noble Lady, who performs the activity of the buddhas of all three times,
Swift heroine, blue-green, with one face and two hands,
Mother who holds an utpala flower—may all be auspicious! ^1-213

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-214]]

Victorious mother Tara—with your noble form,
Your entourage, your lifespan and pure-land,
And your fine and excellent marks—
May I and all others become just like you! ^1-214

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^1-215]]

Through the power of our praise and supplication,
Bestow upon each one of us, no matter where we be,
Freedom from illness, from harm, destitution, and conflict,
And make the Dharma and auspiciousness increase! ^1-215

## The second: daily practice ^2-0

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-1]]

<small>Second, the daily practice. It is good if a support of the Noble Lady is available; if not, visualize it mentally. Arrange the mandala and offerings.</small> ^2-1

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-2]]

In the sky before me, Noble Tara is present in actuality. ^2-2

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-3]]

Namo. In the Noble Lady, embodiment of all the Three Jewels,
I and all beings take refuge.
Arousing bodhicitta in aspiration,
I will enter the profound path! ^2-3

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-4]]

<small>To gather the accumulations, recite:</small> ^2-4

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-5]]

To the noble Lady Tara,
And to all the conquerors and their heirs
Who reside in the ten directions and three times,
Sincerely and with delight, I pay homage. ^2-5

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-6]]

I offer flowers and incense, lamps and perfume,
Food, music and the like,
Both materially present and mentally created.
May they be accepted by the Noble Lady’s assembly! ^2-6

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-7]]

Throughout beginningless time until this very day,
I have committed the ten non-virtues and the five without interval.
I apologize for each and every evil deed committed
Under the influence of disturbed states of mind. ^2-7

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-8]]

I rejoice in all merit and goodness
Created throughout the three times
By shravakas, pratyeka-buddhas and bodhisattvas,
By ordinary beings, and all others. ^2-8

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-9]]

In accordance with the capabilities
And individual aspirations of sentient beings,
I ask you to turn the wheel of Dharma
Of the general, greater and lesser vehicles. ^2-9

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-10]]

Until samsara has been emptied,
Do not pass into nirvana, but please, in your compassion,
Care for each and every being
Sinking in the ocean of suffering. ^2-10

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-11]]

May whatever merit I have gathered
Become a cause for enlightenment,
And may I, in the near future,
Become a splendid guide for sentient beings. ^2-11

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-12]]

<small>To bless the offerings:</small> ^2-12

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-13]]

om benza amrita kundali hana hana hung pé ^2-13

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-14]]

om sobhava shuddho sarva dharma sobhava shuddho hang ^2-14

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-15]]

<small>Thereby cleanse and purify.</small> ^2-15

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-16]]

Here, in the Realm of Lotus Array,
Perfectly formed and abundantly decorated
There are offerings surpassing thought—
Divine articles created from samadhi. ^2-16

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-17]]

om benza argham padam pushpé düpé aloké gendhé naividyé shapda ah hung ^2-17

om benza saparana kham ^2-18

Perfected in an instant of recollection,༔
I am vividly present in the form of the Noble Lady.༔
Upon the moon in my heart center,༔

For as long as I may honor you,༔
Blessed One, please remain seated!༔ ^2-22

pema kamala ya stam༔ ^2-23

These offering clouds of Samantabhadra,༔
Lavishly filling the expanse of dharmadhatu—༔
I offer to Noble Tara and her retinue!༔ ^2-24

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-25]]

Om ah Hung.༔ The three realms, environment and inhabitants, all richness and splendor,༔

I offer to the lords of compassion.༔
Accept them all and bestow your blessings, I pray!༔ ^2-27

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-28]]

om sarva tathagata ratna mandala pudza ho ^2-28

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-29]]

<small>With that, make the specific mandala offering and recite the King of Praises</small> ^2-29

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-30]]

Om. Homage to the noble lady Tara! ^2-30

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-31]]

Homage to Tara, swift and gallant;
Homage to Tuttara, banishing fear;
Homage to Turé, all needs fulfilling;
With svaha we offer you honor and praise! ^2-31

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-32]]

Homage to Tara, swift and gallant,
With glance that flashes like flares of lightning;
Born on the heart of a blossoming lotus
That rose from the tears of the Triple-World’s Lord. ^2-32

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-33]]

Homage to you, your countenance glowing
Like a hundred full moons gathered in autumn,
Lustrous and smiling with brilliant radiance,
Like a thousand stars clustered, ablaze. ^2-33

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-34]]

Homage, golden lady, your lotus-hand
Is graced with a water flower, blue in color;
You are generosity, perseverance, diligence,
Serenity, patience and meditation. ^2-34

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-35]]

Homage to you, whose victories are endless,
The jewel on the great Tathagata’s crown;
You are well served by the heirs of the Victors,
Those who’ve accomplished all the perfections. ^2-35

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-36]]

Homage to you, who with tuttara and hung
Fill the realms of desire to the ends of space;
You trample underfoot the seven worlds,
summoning all at your strong command. your. ^2-36

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-37]]

Homage to you, praised by Indra,
By Agni, Brahma, Maruts, and Shiva;
All the hosts of bhutas, vetalas,
Gandharvas and yakshas pay tribute to you. ^2-37

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-38]]

Homage to you, who with tré and pé
Crush the enemies’ yantras to dust;
With right leg bent in and left leg extended,
Shining, you stamp amid flames wildly blazing. ^2-38

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-39]]

Homage to Ture, lady most fearsome,
You who destroy the most powerful demons;
With lotus-face and a deep-furrowed brow,
You’re the slayer of each and every foe. ^2-39

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-40]]

Homage to you, your fingers gracing
Your heart and displaying the Three Jewels mudra;
Graced with wheels adorning every direction,
Your dazzling radiance overwhelms all. ^2-40

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-41]]

Homage to you, supremely joyous,
Your splendorous crown spreading garlands of light;
Smiling and laughing, your cry of tuttaré
Means demons and worlds all fall into line. ^2-41

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-42]]

Homage lady, you who can summon
All the hosts of earthly guardians;
With your quivering frown and the syllable Hung
You deliver us all from every misfortune. ^2-42

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-43]]

Homage to you, so brightly adorned,
With a sliver of moon as the crest on your crown;
Your locks are graced by Amitabha,
Whose gleaming rays stream endlessly on. ^2-43

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-44]]

Homage to you, in a halo seated,
Blazing with flames, apocalyptic;
Your right leg stretched out and left bent inward,
Immersed in joy, you crush legions of foes. ^2-44

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-45]]

Homage to you, as on the earth’s surface
You strike your palms and stamp your feet;
Your brow deeply furrowed, with Hung you smash
The netherworlds seven and grind them to dust. ^2-45

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-46]]

Homage to you, blissful, gracious and tranquil,
You whose domain is the peace of nirvana;
With om and soha in perfect union,
You lay to waste every terrible evil. ^2-46

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-47]]

Homage to you, immersed in rapture;
You shatter the bodies of all your foes;
you appear from the wisdom-syllable hung,
And display, in full, your ten-syllable mantra. ^2-47

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-48]]

Homage to Turé, your feet stomping boldly,
Formed from the seed of the syllable Hung;
The mountains of Meru, Mandara and Vindhya,
And all the three worlds, they quake and tremble. ^2-48

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-49]]

Homage to you, who hold in your hand
A deer-marked moon, a lake divine;
With tara twice and then with pé,
You totally cleanse all toxins and poisons. ^2-49

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-50]]

Homage to you, whom kings love to serve–
Kings of hosts of gods and kimnaras;
Clad in your armor of joy and splendor,
You clear away nightmares, soothe away strife. ^2-50

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-51]]

Homage to you, eyes shining and lustrous,
Bright with the fullness of sun and moon;
With twice-uttered hara and then with tuttaré
You pacify even the cruelest diseases. ^2-51

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-52]]

Homage to you with the power to liberate,
You who demonstrate threefold reality;
Turé supreme, you completely destroy
The hordes of grahas, vetalas, and yakshas. ^2-52

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-53]]

This Praise with the twenty-one verses of homage
Is, in itself, the root mantra. ^2-53

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-54]]

<small>Recite even times.</small> ^2-54

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-55]]

The wise who have true devotion for this goddess,
And who chant this at dusk ^2-55

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-56]]

Or when rising at dawn—
Just by bringing it to mind, they are granted total courage.
It pacifies every misdeed,
Overcomes all roads to the lower realms, ^2-56

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-57]]

And quickly brings empowerment
From seven million conquerers.
In this life, one achieves superior qualities;
And ultimately, one arrives at the state of the buddhas. ^2-57

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-58]]

By recalling it, even dreadful poisons,
Locally found or transported from elsewhere,
Whether ingested or imbibed,
Will be completely expelled. ^2-58

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-59]]

Harm from evil, affliction from plague or venom—
All such miseries are eliminated
For other sentient beings too. ^2-59

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-60]]

By chanting it twice, thrice or seven times,
Those wanting children will have them,
Those wanting wealth will be prosperous,
One’s every wish will be fulfilled,
Obstacles will vanish, and every problem will be overcome. ^2-60

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-61]]

<small>Thus recite it together with its nenefits.</small> ^2-61

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-62]]

<small>Then joining your palms, recite:</small> ^2-62

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-63]]

Sublime and noble Lady, with your retinue,
Lovingly regard me with compassion, beyond the bounds of thought!
Bless me so that no obstacles block
The fulfillment of all my prayers! ^2-63

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-64]]

May the Buddha’s teachings spread and flourish!
May the holders of the teachings live in harmony and good health!
May obstacles that threaten them subside!
May their activities of teaching and practice prosper! ^2-64

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-65]]

May all sickness and famine, fighting and strife, recede!
May spiritual richness increase yet further!
May the spiritual rulers’ kingdoms expand!
May every country near and far be in harmony! ^2-65

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-66]]

Protect us from untimely death and the sixteen threats,
From menacing dreams and sinister omens,
From the miseries of samsara’s lower realms,
From every peril, now and always! ^2-66

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-67]]

Increase our life, merit, capacity, experience and realization!
May harmful notions not intrude!
May the twofold awakened mind arise, free of effort!
May our aims be fulfilled in accordance with the Dharma! ^2-67

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-68]]

From now until supreme enlightenment,
Like a mother protects her only child,
Safeguard us always with your kindness!
Let us be indivisible from you! ^2-68

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-69]]

<small>With this extraordinary prayer for your desired aims, invoke the oath.</small> ^2-69

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-70]]

The front visualization melts into light and dissolves into me.
Blessed to be indivisible from her,
I turn into the form of Noble Tara,
Visible yet insubstantial. ^2-70

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-71]]

<small>While you keep in mind the vivid presence and pride of being the Noble Lady yourself, recite the ten syllable mantra as many times as you can.</small> ^2-71

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-72]]

om taré tuttaré turé soha ^2-72

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-73]]

<small>Then recite the hundred-syllable mantra.</small> ^2-73

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-74]]

om benza sato samaya manupalaya benza sato tenopa tishta dridho mé bhava sutohyo mé bhava supohyo mé bhava anurakto mé bhava sarva siddhi mé prayatsa sarva karma su tsa mé tsittam shri yang kuru hung ha ha ha ha ho bhagavan sarva tatagata benza ma mé muntsa benzi bhava maha samaya sato ah ^2-74

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-75]]

For anything lacking or incomplete,
Or beyond my ability to perform—
Whatever mistakes I have committed,
Please bear witness and forgive them all! ^2-75

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-76]]

As the deity is indivisible from my own mind,
I settle within the original state of dharmadhatu. ^2-76

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-77]]

<small>Thus, foster the view of the true nature.</small> ^2-77

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-78]]

Through this virtue may I quickly
Accomplish the state of Noble Tara.
And may I bring each and every being,
Without a single exception, to that very same state. ^2-78

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-79]]

<small>Dedicate the merit and make noble aspirations.</small> ^2-79

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-80]]

Like the wish-fulfilling jewel and the vase of bounty,
Unimpeded, every wish you fulfill.
Noble Tara, conquerors and your heirs,
Bestow the auspiciousness that we may be forever nurtured by your compassion. ^2-80

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-81]]

<small>With that, utter verses of auspiciousness and toss flowers.</small> ^2-81

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-82]]

Noble and compassionate Tara, we pray to you:
May we beings, all of us, infinite in number,
May we purify the two obscurations, swiftly perfect the two accumulations,
And so attain the state of perfect awakening! ^2-82

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-83]]

Until then, throughout all of our lives,
May we find supreme happiness among gods and humans!
May all obstacles to the attainment of omniscience,
All harmful forces, all sickness and disease, ^2-83

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-84]]

All that brings untimely death,
All bad dreams and evil omens,
The eight dangers and all harm—
May all be swiftly pacified and purged! ^2-84

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-85]]

May auspiciousness, happiness, and prosperity,
Both worldly and transcendent,
Flourish and expand, and may all our wishes
Be effortlessly and spontaneously fulfilled! ^2-85

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-86]]

May we endeavor in the practice, may the Dharma spread,
May we always meditate on you, and behold your perfect face;
May we realize the nature of emptiness, and may precious bodhicitta
Develop and expand, just like the waxing moon! ^2-86

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-87]]

May I be reborn in a beautiful lotus flower,
In that excellent and joyous buddha-realm,
And may the Buddha Amitabha himself
Grant me the prophecy foretelling my enlightenment! ^2-87

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-88]]

Divine goddess whom I’ve accomplished in previous lives,
Noble Lady, who performs the activity of the buddhas of all three times,
Swift heroine, blue-green, with one face and two hands,
Mother who holds an utpala flower—may all be auspicious! ^2-88

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-89]]

Victorious mother Tara—with your noble form,
Your entourage, your lifespan and pure-land,
And your fine and excellent marks—
May I and all others become just like you! ^2-89

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^2-90]]

Through the power of our praise and supplication,
Bestow upon each one of us, no matter where we be,
Freedom from illness, from harm, destitution, and conflict,
And make the Dharma and auspiciousness increase! ^2-90

## The Tambura of Devotion: A Supplication Invokingthe Crucial Essence of the Vows of the Noble Lady Wish-Fulfilling Wheel ^a-0

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^a-1]]

From the vast lake of Avalokiteśvara’s compassion,
Awater lily of benefit and happiness bloomed gloriously:
Noble Tārā, shining with the light of nectar—
Dispel the terrifying forces of darkness! ^a-1

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^a-2]]

Mother of all buddhas, balm that heals my pain,
Loving nurse, only friend to all beings,
In all my lifetimes, you have been my most sublime protector.
Until the heart of awakening,I rely on you for refuge! ^a-2

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^a-3]]

Beings like me, tormented by karma,
Wherever we are born, are constantly plagued
By illness, war, famine, and so on.
Drag me from this swamp of suffering, O Noble Lady! ^a-3

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^a-4]]

Like ripples on water, my thoughts follow habitual patterns,
And every single moment I create
Many causes for lower rebirths.
Eradicate these endless seeds of saṃsāra right away, O Noble Lady! ^a-4

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^a-5]]

Since thus far I have failed to gather the great accumulations,
Although I pride myself on having faithfully entered the Buddhadharma,
I am unable to entrust myself to the Three Jewels.
Rip out these karmic obscurations from their root, O Noble Lady! ^a-5

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^a-6]]

Even if I see it ripen right before my eyes,
I still don’t believe in the karmic law of cause and effect.
I’m just a wild beast clad in saffron robes.
Lead my mindto virtue, O Noble Lady! ^a-6

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^a-7]]

Day and night, moment by moment, my body decays.
Until I have fulfilled the great purpose of this excellent human life,
Cut the lasso of the Lord of Death, so difficult to escape,
With your sword of wisdom, O Noble Lady! ^a-7

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^a-8]]

In every situation, quickly protect mefrom the eight or sixteen dangers—
Evil forces, epidemics, poison, weapons,
Sudden death at the hands of wicked people,
Fires, floods, or wild animals, O Noble Lady! ^a-8

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^a-9]]

As long as self-clinging and bad habits are present in my mindstream,
The river of suffering will continue to flow.
Thus, I should know the nature of the skandhas and so on.
Light the lamp of that understanding for me, O Noble Lady! ^a-9

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^a-10]]

Recognition of beings as my mothers, love, and compassion
Are qualities I should not just talk about;
Heroic bodhisattvas exchange their happiness for others’ suffering.
Help me to muster suchvast courage, O Noble Lady! ^a-10

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^a-11]]

In all my future lives, may I meet an authentic teacher
And fully understand all teachings they impart.
Destroy Māra’s army of obstacles to life and liberation
Until I reach awakening, O Noble Lady! ^a-11

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^a-12]]

Grant me the profound wisdom to see with pure eyes,
A good character with little inclination to afflictive mental states,
Which fosters deep devotion to spiritual training with mindfulness and alertness.
Mergemy mind with the Dharma, O Noble Lady! ^a-12

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^a-13]]

When the karma that keeps me in this life is spent,
Ease the agonies of death and help me realize the eleven thoughts;
Show me mytrue nature, luminosity, with decisive recognition of the dhātu,
Free from the extremes of origination and cessation, O Noble Lady! ^a-13

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^a-14]]

Even if the bardo arises for just a fleeting instant,
Let my pure perception ofthe emanation and dissolution of the maṇḍala be complete.
Take me to Abhirati, the lotus-arrayed realm of Sukhāvatī,
Or any other buddha field—O Noble Lady! ^a-14

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^a-15]]

Unless I consciously decide to be born in saṃsāra,
Don’t let me circle helplessly here due to my karma.
Guide me toBuddha Boundless Life and Light
To receive his prophecy, O Noble Lady! ^a-15

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^a-16]]

In the vast ocean of realms,whether there are buddhas or not,
May I guard the sublime Dharma with my life
And fully teach the excellent conduct of the bodhisattvas.
Please fulfill this wish of mine, O Noble Lady! ^a-16

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^a-17]]

While in this body, may all the misdeeds, karmic obscurations, and transgressions
I have accumulated since time immemorial be purified,
And until then, let me be in harmony with the Dharma,
And grant the siddhi of immortality, O Noble Lady! ^a-17

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^a-18]]

Even in my dreams, may bodhicitta never wane,
And may I fully turn away from Māra’s machinations.
Let all my actions of body, speech, and mind be a powerful source
Of benefit and prosperity for the teachings and beings, O Noble Lady! ^a-18

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^a-19]]

Let me leave behind the eight worldly dharmas and distractions of this life,
So that in the solitude of the woods, unbothered by obstacles,
I may concentrate on meditation practicealone.
In this way, make this human life meaningful, O Noble Lady! ^a-19

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^a-20]]

Let this duḥkha I’m experiencing right now
Quickly exhaust the causes and effects
Of all living beings’ sufferings,
And guide us from happiness to happiness, O Noble Lady! ^a-20

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^a-21]]

Let the supreme Buddhadharma, source of benefit and happiness,
Spread and flourish in all ten directions without ever waning,
And please prolong the lives of all the great masters
Who upholdit, O Noble Lady! ^a-21

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^a-22]]

Especially, grant all beings the good fortune to hear
The profound truth of the definitive essence,
And lift up with praise to heavenly heights
The hosts of devas, nāgas, and yakṣaswell disposed to it, O Noble Lady! ^a-22

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^a-23]]

In all countries, let the word “unhappiness” never be heard again,
And from the vast jewel cloud of a golden age of happiness and joy,
Filled with longevity, merit, prosperity, and devotion to the teachings,
Let a rain of all I could possibly wish for fall, O Noble Lady! ^a-23

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^a-24]]

Burn the thicket of misguided thoughts and deeds
Of all humans and non-humans who inflict harm
Upon the Buddhadharma and happiness of beings
In the fires of your power, O Noble Lady! ^a-24

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^a-25]]

Free my mothers, infinite in number, from the fear of untimely death,
And let them spontaneously enjoy the glory of the ten virtues.
Grounded in the Three Jewels and the trainings,
Steer their course upward to liberation, O Noble Lady! ^a-25

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^a-26]]

Especially, don’t let me and those who depend on me
Ever fall under the sway of obstacles,
And plant the pillar of long and stable life
So that our lives and practice reach completion, O Noble Lady! ^a-26

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^a-27]]

Goddess to whom I entrust my heart in this and all my future lives,
Bhagavatī, mother of all buddhas of the three times,
If you do not fulfill my aspirations,
No one else can help me, O Noble Lady! ^a-27

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^a-28]]

You attained the inconceivable body of space
Through the power of the two accumulations gathered throughout beginningless time,
So when I supplicate you with one-pointed, fervent yearning,
Let your blessings and compassion be unconstrained, O Noble Lady! ^a-28

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^a-29]]

At the center of a lotus, your body shines like the full autumn moon,
Flush with the youthful vigor of vajra bliss.
With the boundless halo ofyour stainless light,
Grant the four enlightened activities, O Noble Lady! ^a-29

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^a-30]]

With your palm, formed from a hundred merits,
Which grants the supreme gift of anything one could wish for—
Dharma, wealth, sense pleasures, and liberation—to whomever it touches,
Caress my head, O Noble Lady! ^a-30

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^a-31]]

From the exquisite, freshly opened lotus
Atop the precious stem between your thumb and ring finger
Drips the wish-granting honey of supreme and common siddhis.
Let it fill my mind with bliss, O Noble Lady! ^a-31

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^a-32]]

Your magical appearance is graceful and charming, adorned with strings of pearls,
Small golden bells, divine garments, and ornaments,
Ablaze with the thousandfold splendor of the molten gold of sunrise.
Reveal yourself before my faithful eyes, O Noble Lady! ^a-32

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^a-33]]

Sublime giver of rest in infinite pure realms,
With the stainless full moon behind you,
Seated in indestructible vajra posture,
Adorn the crown of my head, O Noble Lady! ^a-33

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^a-34]]

With your Brahmā voice, please teach
The profound and vast Dharma without cease,
Cut the web of the three obscurations,
And relieve us at the victorious level of awakening, O Noble Lady! ^a-34

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^a-35]]

In short, since you are my essential refuge,
Until I reach awakening,
Never let me fall under the sway of the obstacle-maker Māra,
And grant your grace so I bring the twofold benefit to completion! ^a-35

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^a-36]]

<small>At the age of thirty I, Yönten Gyatso,formulated this spontaneous prayer while I was severely ill, andthe country, too, was suffering from decline and degeneration.By the truth of the Three Jewels, may it be answered! Translated by Khyentse Vision Project (Heidi Nevin and Jamie Creek)</small> ^a-36

## The Sweet-Sounding Song That Fulfills All Aims ^b-0

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^b-1]]

OṂ TĀRE TUTTĀRE TURE SVĀHĀ ^b-1

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^b-2]]

With OṂ the jewel of liberation and all that could be wished for is bestowed.
With the excellence of splendor, prosperity, and the like,
You bring healing to existence and peace, mistress of the three secrets,
Noble Mother of perfect generosity, to you I pay homage! ^b-2

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^b-3]]

With TĀRE, the precious vessel of bodhicitta,
You constantly liberate living beings, who are in number as extensive as space,
From the ocean of suffering in cyclic existence and the lower realms.
Noble Mother Tārā, to you I pay homage! ^b-3

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^b-4]]

With TUTTĀRE, the ambrosia of blessings
Banishes the tormenting heat of fear and misery,
And causes the lotus of benefit and well-being to bloom.
To you, Mother, who protects from distress, I pay homage! ^b-4

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^b-5]]

TURE—the instant I remember you, Venerable One,
With your display of attributes and forms to tame beings as appropriate,
You protect against all the misfortunes of belief in a self.
I pay homage to you, compassionate and swift Mother. ^b-5

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^b-6]]

SVĀHĀ—bountiful clouds of enlightened activity
Rain down jewels of virtue and goodness
That bring relief to all beings of the three realms.
I pay homage to you, Mother, Wish-fulfilling Wheel. ^b-6

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^b-7]]

In brief, to you, the embodiment of the primordial wisdom
Of all the victorious ones and their spiritual heirs,
Noble Lady, I pray with heartfelt devotion,
Take care of me until enlightenment. ^b-7

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^b-8]]

Pacify all circumstances that prevent the accomplishment of the sacred Dharma.
Grant your blessings that, with all favorable conditions in abundance,
Long life, merit, splendor, knowledge, and love,
I may accomplish the twofold benefit—for myself and others. ^b-8

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^b-9]]

<small>Jamyang Khyentse Wangpo, who has one-pointed, heartfelt devotion for the supreme female deity, composed this prayer. May it be accomplished accordingly! </small> ^b-9

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^c-0]]

## The Excellent Bilva Tree of the Accomplishment of All Aims: Verses of Homage to the Noble Lady Tārā Wish-Fulfilling Wheel. ^c-0

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^c-1]]

Namo Āryā Tārāye ^c-1

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^c-2]]

The stainless jewel ship of your compassion
Liberates beings from the ocean of existence and peace,
Bringing them relief in the city of the heart of awakening—
O Noble Lady, Wish-Fulfilling Wheel, to you I pay homage. ^c-2

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^c-3]]

The mere appearance in my mind of the moonlight of your marks and signs,
That finest essence churned from the ocean of the two accumulations,
Grants the easeful rest of longevity and wisdom—
O Noble Lady, Wish-Fulfilling Wheel, to you I pay homage. ^c-3

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^c-4]]

From the single beryl lute of your secret speech,
Music from the ocean of melodious sounds
Appears to all in a melody of a million gathered teachings—
O Noble Lady, Wish-Fulfilling Wheel, to you I pay homage. ^c-4

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^c-5]]

Your mind is the non-dual union of wisdom and basic space,
In which any stirring of the constructs of grasper and grasped has been stilled,
And yet the majesty of your knowledge and love is infinite—
O Noble Lady, Wish-Fulfilling Wheel, to you I pay homage. ^c-5

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^c-6]]

Sublime mother, in your mesmerizing display
Of the sixty-four inexhaustible qualities of freedom and ripening,
You unsteady even the victors and their heirs—
O Noble Lady, Wish-Fulfilling Wheel, to you I pay homage. ^c-6

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^c-7]]

In a continuous stream, pervading all of space,
Your spontaneous and effortless activity,
Like a wish-granting tree, answers the hopes of those to be tamed—
O Noble Lady, Wish-Fulfilling Wheel, to you I pay homage. ^c-7

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^c-8]]

In short, to fully accomplish the sworn resolve
Of all those who enjoy mastery over the ten powers,
You perform unimaginable deeds to tame living beings—
O Noble Lady, Wish-Fulfilling Wheel, to you I pay homage. ^c-8

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^c-9]]

Thus, in devotion I pay homage and supplicate you—
Grant your blessings so that your wisdom insight, like the great clouds of the perfect age,
Showers down a gentle rain of all desired siddhis,
Spreading an abundant feast of joy and benefit. ^c-9

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^c-10]]

<small>For the sake of Gyurme Namdak, one whose expansive vision is rich with insight, Jamyang Khyentse Wangpo composed an auspicious number of verses in homage and supplication to the Noble Lady, Moon of Compassion. </small> ^c-10

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^d-0]]

## Words of Auspiciousness to Accomplish One’s Aims ^d-0

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^d-1]]

Namastāraye! ^d-1

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^d-2]]

By merely hearing your name,
Beings are freed from saṃsāra’s ocean.
Enraptured by this wondrous miracle,
I supplicate you with total devotion. ^d-2

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^d-3]]

Your gorgeous form is adorned with virtuous marks,
Like millions of powdered emeralds piled up high.
May you display this splendid form before my eyes,
With a hundred peacefully smiling expressions. ^d-3

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^d-4]]

From the accomplishment of your secret vidyā mantra,
An ocean of precious treasures,
Siddhis, both mundane and supramundane, well forth.
Thus, may you grant benefit and bliss throughout the three planes. ^d-4

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^d-5]]

Basic space is profound and sublime peace.
It is free of directions and parts.
When bestowing your blessings, at that very moment,
Make duality dissolve into the central channel! ^d-5

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^d-6]]

Most especially, for all of us living in dread of the decline
Of these dark and degenerate times,
Mother of All Victors, day and night I turn to you.
I know no other protection than you. ^d-6

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^d-7]]

So, extend your hand of compassion,
Dispel all my fears,
And grant me the bountiful fortune
Of finding relief in a perfect realm. ^d-7

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^d-8]]

In the meantime, I beseech you, make the qualities of the four perfections
Blossom with blazing beauty,
And laden with such abundance,
The indivisible three kāyas are ultimately unified. ^d-8

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^d-9]]

In short, from now until awakening,
May I remain forever
Under Noble Tārā’s joyful gaze,
And thus may the beautiful picture of all desired aims be perfected. ^d-9

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^d-10]]

<small>These small flowers of auspicious supplication were scattered by the vidyādhara Mañjughoṣa to grant some relief to those living in dread of the decline of these dark times. Grant your blessings that these aspirations be fulfilled! Sarvadā śreyo bhavatu.</small> ^d-10

## The Sage's Powerful Words of Truth: A Prayer for the Spread of the Omniscient Buddha's Teachings ^e-0

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^e-1]]

Homage to the guru and all the buddhas and bodhisattvas! ^e-1

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^e-2]]

Among the thousand buddhas of this fortunate age,
You are the most wondrous and majestic, as rare as an _uḍumbara_ flower,
You who care for our world during this age of conflict,
Mighty sage, Siddhārtha, to you I pay homage! ^e-2

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^e-3]]

All you buddhas, bodhisattvas, śrāvakas and pratyekabuddhas in all directions,
Great bodhisattvas, Mañjuśrī, Avalokiteśvara,
Vajrapāṇi, Maitreya and the rest,
Together with the sixteen sthaviras and seven great patriarchs, ^e-3

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^e-4]]

Six ornaments and two supreme ones of this world, the two marvellous ācāryas,
Eighty-four mahāsiddhas, and the rest,
And especially, the Lotus-born Guru, whose kindness is beyond compare,
Śāntarakṣita, Tri Songdetsen, Vairotsana and Atiśa, ^e-4

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^e-5]]

Five Sakya Patriarchs, Marpa, Milarepa and Gampopa,
Lama Tsongkhapa, Mañjughoṣa in person, and all the rest,
All you great learned and accomplished vidyādharas of India and Tibet,
And all you peaceful and wrathful _yidam_ deities, ḍākinīs and dharmapālas—
We invoke you! Turn your wisdom minds towards us, and look upon us with compassion! ^e-5

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^e-6]]

All the teachings of the Buddha—Vinaya, Sūtra, Abhidharma,
The four classes of tantra within the Secret Mantra,
The teachings of the ten great pillars of the study lineage in the Land of Snows,
And the eight great chariots of the practice lineage,
All the sūtra and mantra traditions of the Buddha’s teachings—
Look now and see the extent to which they have all declined! ^e-6

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^e-7]]

The saffron-robed saṅgha, upholders of the victory banner of discipline and renunciation,
Bodhisattvas who, without pretense or deception, genuinely seek to benefit others,
And holders of awareness mantras who maintain the commitments, and practise generation and completion—
Look how these practitioners have been reduced to pale imitations of the real thing, like a mere drawing of a butter lamp!
Look upon us now with your eyes of wisdom, and care for us in your compassion, we pray!
Beings of this evil age with its five degenerations, ^e-7

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^e-8]]

Are caught in the bonds of turbulent and destructive emotions, their cravings and desires.
The followers of the teachings fight amongst themselves,
Spurred on by the fierce winds of jealousy,
And degenerate views are widespread. ^e-8

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^e-9]]

Through these defilements, brought about by conflicts within the tradition,
The Dharma protectors, guardians of all that is positive, have turned away from us,
While warrior-like _asuras_ and barbarians fill the world—
In these and other ways, the teachings have declined. ^e-9

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^e-10]]

_Kyema Kyihü_! O compassionate protectors!
Increase the power and strength of your sacred commitments!
Why would you be discouraged by the sadness of these evil times? ^e-10

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^e-11]]

Since you are masters of the ten strengths and possessors of the ten powers,
You must surely heal and restore the teachings.
Send us genuine teachers who are learned, disciplined and accomplished,
To breathe fresh life into the Buddhadharma! ^e-11

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^e-12]]

In both India and Tibet, may the precious lamps of joyful celebration,
Be set ablaze, to usher in another golden age,
And may the non-sectarian, lotus-like teachings of the Buddha, flourish and blossom. ^e-12

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^e-13]]

May the minds of those who harbour ill-will towards the teachings be changed,
And filled with inspiration to practise the ten virtuous actions.
May the saṅgha, the holders of the teachings, fill this world of ours,
And study and practice increase and spread. ^e-13

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^e-14]]

May vast clouds of offerings fill all monasteries and temples,
And the great drum of the Dharma resound far and wide,
Annihilating the deluded arguments of the _tīrthikas_,
And sounding the victory of the Buddhadharma in all directions. ^e-14

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^e-15]]

May the lives of all the holders of the teachings be secure,
And may the lives and merits of benefactors increase,
May even the names of those who would harm the teachings disappear,
And may the victory banner of the teachings be raised in all directions! ^e-15

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^e-16]]

<small>Chökyi Lodrö wrote down whatever came to mind at the foot of the bodhi tree in Vajrāsana (Bodhgayā) in the noble land of India. May these words be blessed so that they all come true. Sarvadā maṅgalaṃ! Translated by Adam Pearcey, 2007.</small> ^e-16

![[1-SOURCES/Sadhana/bo-ཟབ་ཏིག་སྒྲོལ་ཆོག.md#^g-6]]

In all my lifetimes,
May I carry the great burden of the teachings.
Even if I am unable to carry that great burden,
The worry that the teachings may decline—
May at least a little of it weigh on my mind. ^g-6
